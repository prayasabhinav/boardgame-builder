# Spud — catch the ball, freeze the rest

- From: Spud, a street ball game
- Players: 4 or more players
- Time: 15 min
- Made by: Traditional; played in the United States
- Rules from: The Big Book of Rules, p. 194

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

A caller bounces a ball high and calls a name. The player named runs for the ball while the others flee. When the ball is caught, the others freeze, and the catcher throws at one of them.

## What it shows a designer

A hit or a miss gives a letter to someone, so each throw has a cost whichever way it goes. It shows how a game with no winner can still be played hard because everyone wants to avoid the last letter.

## What you need

- a ball that bounces high
- a wide flat space
- paper to note letters

## How to play

1. **Call.** The caller bounces the ball hard and calls the name of one player.
2. **Run.** The named player runs to catch the ball. Everyone else runs away from it.
3. **Freeze.** When the ball is caught, the catcher shouts Spud. Everyone else freezes where they are.
4. **Throw.** The catcher may take three steps towards a frozen player, then throws the ball at that player. If it hits, that player gets a letter. If it misses, the catcher gets the letter.
5. **Letters.** Letters spell SPUD. The player who got the letter is the next caller.
6. **End.** A player who gets all four letters has lost. Everyone else is safe.

## After playing, talk about

- How did you choose your target?
- Why might a player aim carefully rather than throw hard?
- How is a game with only one loser different from a game with one winner?
