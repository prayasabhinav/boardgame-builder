# Slapjack — be first to slap the jack

- From: Slapjack, a card reflex game
- Players: 2 or more players
- Time: 5–10 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Big Book of Rules, p. 338
- This version: A player with no cards left is out

Players take turns turning cards face up into one pile. When a jack appears, everyone slaps the pile. The fastest hand wins the whole pile.

## What it shows a designer

Turns decide who plays a card, but speed decides who gets the prize. It shows how a game can mix two kinds of timing, ordered turns and a free-for-all, in one rhythm.

## What you need

- a deck of 52 playing cards

## How to play

1. **Deal.** Deal out all the cards. Each player stacks theirs face down without looking.
2. **Turn a card.** In turn, each player takes the top card of their stack and puts it face up in the middle.
3. **Slap.** When a jack is turned up, everyone races to slap the pile. The first hand down wins the pile.
4. **Collect.** Put the pile on the bottom of your stack. Play restarts with the next player.
5. **Out and win.** A player with no cards is out. The last player with cards wins.

## After playing, talk about

- How did you get your hand ready without slapping too soon?
- What happened when two people slapped together?
- Who was fastest, and does speed alone win here?
