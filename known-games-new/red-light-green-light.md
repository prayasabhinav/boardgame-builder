# Red Light, Green Light — run, walk or freeze

- From: Red Light, Green Light (Stop and Go), a children's running game
- Players: 3 or more players
- Time: 10 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Big Book of Rules, p. 168

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

One player is It and stands far away with their back turned. The others try to reach It. It calls green, yellow or red light, and the players move or freeze to match.

## What it shows a designer

One caller controls the speed of the whole group. It shows how a single hidden moment, the turn round, can make every runner choose between speed and safety.

## What you need

- an open space about thirty steps long
- a line to mark the start

## How to play

1. **Line up.** Choose one player to be It. It stands thirty steps from the others, facing away. The others line up on the start line.
2. **Call.** It calls one of three commands. Green light means run towards It. Yellow light means walk. Red light means freeze before It turns round.
3. **Turn.** After red light, It turns round. If It sees anyone moving, that player goes back to the start line.
4. **Cheating call.** If It turns before saying red light, all the other players take one giant step forward.
5. **Win.** The first player to touch It wins and is It for the next game.

## After playing, talk about

- When did you run, and when did you hold back?
- How did your choice change when you were near It?
- What would make the game harder for the caller?

## Its blocks

- Core mechanic: call and response; stop on a signal
- Victory condition: race to finish
- Player interaction: solo in parallel
- Chance or skill: mostly skill
- Information visibility: full information
- Timing: no turns
