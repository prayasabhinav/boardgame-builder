# Indian Dice — poker with five dice

- From: Indian Dice, a dice game with five dice
- Players: 2–6 players
- Time: 5–10 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: Board and Table Games from Many Civilizations, p. 135
- This version: Hands of the same kind are compared by number, and a tie is shared; a throw with no pair is the lowest hand

Each player throws five dice up to three times, keeping the dice they like and throwing the rest again. The highest set of matching dice wins. Aces can count as any number you like.

## What it shows a designer

Letting a player keep some dice and throw the rest gives them a little control over luck. It shows how one choice, how many times to throw, can change the pace of the whole game, and how a first player who sets the number of throws shapes what everyone else may do.

## What you need

- 5 dice
- paper and a pen to note who has the highest hand

## How to play

1. **Order.** Each player throws one die. The highest goes first, then the next highest, and so on.
2. **Know the numbers.** Sixes count highest and twos count lowest. Aces are wild: an ace counts as any number you choose.
3. **First player chooses.** The first player decides to take one, two or three throws. Every other player gets the same number of throws.
4. **Throw.** Throw all five dice. After the first throw you may set any dice aside and throw the others again, or throw all five again. After the second throw you may throw any dice again, including ones you set aside.
5. **Hands.** From highest to lowest: five of a kind (five sixes beat five fives, and so on), four of a kind, a full house (three of one number and a pair), three of a kind, two pairs, one pair.
6. **Win.** After everyone has played, the highest-ranked hand wins. If two hands are the same kind, the one with higher numbers wins. If they are still equal, the players share the win.

## After playing, talk about

- When did you keep a pair, and when did you throw everything again?
- How did the first player's choice of one, two or three throws change what you did?
- Where did the aces help you most?
