# Pig — pass cards fast and touch your nose

- From: Pig (the card game), a party game
- Players: 3–13 players
- Time: 5–10 min a round
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: Hoyle's Rules of Games, under 'Pig'
- This version: Not the dice game called Pig. A forfeit is optional

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

Everyone has four cards and passes one to the left at the same time, again and again. The first to hold four cards of one rank quietly puts a finger on their nose. As each other player notices, they do the same. The last to notice is the Pig.

## What it shows a designer

The cards decide who can finish, but attention decides who loses. It shows how a game can be a race where the cards and the people watching each other count together.

## What you need

- a pack of cards
- a table or a circle of chairs

## How to play

1. **Pick the cards.** For each player, take four cards of one rank. With seven players, take the aces up to the sevens. Shuffle.
2. **Deal.** Deal four cards to each player, one at a time.
3. **Pass.** Everyone picks one card from their hand and puts it to their left, then takes up the card that was put to their right. Do not wait; keep passing.
4. **Four of a kind.** The first player to hold four cards of one rank stops passing and puts a finger on their nose.
5. **Notice.** Each other player puts a finger on their nose when they see it. Do it as quickly as you can, and keep playing until you do.
6. **The Pig.** The last to notice is the Pig. Agree a small forfeit if you like.

## After playing, talk about

- Did you watch your cards or the other players more?
- How did you choose which card to pass?
- Why did the Pig lose, when the cards were equal for everyone?
