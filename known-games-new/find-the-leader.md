# Find the Leader — copy without being seen

- From: Find the Leader, a guessing game with a hidden leader
- Players: 6 or more players
- Time: 10 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Big Book of Rules, p. 148

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

One player leaves the room. The others choose a leader and all copy the leader's movements. The outsider returns and has to guess who the leader is.

## What it shows a designer

The group works as one without a signal. It shows how a hidden role (the leader) can be shared with an audience, while one player sees only the effect.

## What you need

- a circle of players
- a room with a door

## How to play

1. **Send out.** One player, the outsider, leaves the room. The others pick a leader.
2. **Start moving.** The leader starts an action, such as tapping a foot or scratching a head. Everyone copies at once.
3. **Return.** The outsider comes back and watches the group. The leader changes the action from time to time, and the others follow without looking at the leader too often.
4. **Guess.** The outsider tries to name the leader.
5. **Next.** When the outsider guesses right, the leader leaves the room next.

## After playing, talk about

- How did the group follow the leader without giving them away?
- What would the outsider look for?
- How many guesses seem fair, and why?

## Its blocks

- Core mechanic: call and response
- Victory condition: one loser
- Chance or skill: mostly skill
- Information visibility: hidden identity
- Timing: no turns
