# Gin Rummy — keep your sets hidden in your hand

- From: Gin Rummy, a Rummy game
- Players: 2–4 players
- Time: 15–20 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Everything Games Book, p. 130
- This version: Play to 50 points (two hands) instead of 100

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

You build sets inside your own hand and never lay them down. Each turn you take one card and throw one away. The first player whose whole hand fits in sets, with one card spare, calls out Gin.

## What it shows a designer

Nothing is shown until the end, so the only clue others have is what you take and what you throw. It shows how hiding progress makes a race feel quiet and tense.

## What you need

- a standard deck of 52 cards
- paper and a pencil to keep score

## How to play

1. **Deal.** Deal ten cards to each player. Put the rest face down as the stock and turn the top card face up to start the discard pile.
2. **Know the sets.** A run is three or more cards of one suit in order. A lay is three or four cards of the same rank.
3. **Take.** On your turn, take the top card of the stock or the top card of the discard pile. Only the top card, never the whole pile.
4. **Discard.** Put one card you do not need on the discard pile. Your turn ends and the player on your left goes next.
5. **Keep sets in hand.** Keep your runs and lays in your hand. Do not lay them on the table.
6. **Gin.** When every card but one is in a set, put the last card face down on the discard pile and say "Gin." The hand ends.
7. **Score.** The player who called Gin scores 25 points. Play to 50 points.

## After playing, talk about

- What did you learn about other players from the cards they took?
- How long did you hold a card you could not use yet?
- Would the game feel different if sets were laid on the table?
