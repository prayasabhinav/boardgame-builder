# Zohn Ahl — two runners, one river

- From: Zohn Ahl, a game of the Kiowa people of Oklahoma
- Players: 2 players, or 2 teams
- Time: 15–20 min
- Made by: Traditional; played by Kiowa women and girls, drawn on the ground with stones
- Rules from: Board and Table Games from Many Civilizations, p. 5
- This version: A ring of 40 spaces drawn on paper, four coins for the dice sticks, and three laps instead of play until one side holds every counter

Each team has one runner. The two runners go round a ring in opposite directions, so they meet. The throws of four sticks decide how far a runner goes. A torrent on the track sends a runner back, and every lap, meeting or fall in the water moves counters from one team to the other.

## What it shows a designer

Nobody chooses a move here, yet the game is full of things that happen to the two sides. It shows how a game of pure luck can still have a story if the track has a river, a meeting point and a bank of counters changing hands.

## What you need

- a sheet of paper
- four coins; mark one with a dot, because it is the special stick
- one counter for each runner
- eight counters or beans for the bank, four for each team

## How to play

1. **Draw the ring.** Draw a ring of 40 spaces. Mark the bottom space START. Mark the top space as the torrent, and the spaces on the far left and the far right as dry streams. Each team gets four bank counters.
2. **Teams and runners.** One runner for each team. Both start at START. One runs clockwise and the other runs anticlockwise.
3. **Throw.** Teams throw in turn. Toss all four coins. Count the heads. One head scores 1, two score 2, three score 3, four score 6, and no heads score 10. If the dotted coin is among the heads on a score of 1 or 3, throw again.
4. **Move.** Move your runner round the ring by the score.
5. **The torrent.** A runner who stops on the torrent goes back to START, and its team pays one counter to the other team.
6. **The dry streams.** A runner who stops on a dry stream misses its team's next throw.
7. **Meeting.** If a runner stops on the same space as the other runner, the one who arrived last sends the other back to START and wins a counter from the other team.
8. **Laps.** The first runner back round to START wins a counter from the other team and the lap ends. If the throw takes the runner past START, it moves the extra points along the next lap.
9. **The end.** After three laps, the team with more counters wins. If one team holds all eight counters sooner, it wins at once.

## After playing, talk about

- No one chose a move. What was left to play for?
- What did the torrent and the dry streams change about how the two runners felt?
- If you could give each team one choice a turn, where would you put it?
