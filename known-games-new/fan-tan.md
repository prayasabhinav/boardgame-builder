# Fan Tan — build the sevens outwards

- From: Fan Tan (also called Stops or Sevens), a traditional card game
- Players: 3–6 players
- Time: 15–20 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: Hoyle's Rules of Games, under 'Fan Tan'
- This version: No chips. A player who cannot play simply passes. The first player out wins.

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

The whole deck is dealt out. The four sevens start four rows. Each row grows up and down from its seven in suit order. The first player to get rid of every card wins.

## What it shows a designer

A shared layout that everyone builds on, with hands nobody can see. It shows how holding a card back can block other players.

## What you need

- one deck of 52 playing cards
- a table with room for four rows of cards

## How to play

1. **Deal.** Deal every card out, one at a time. It does not matter if some players have one card more.
2. **Open with a seven.** The player to the left of the dealer must play a seven or pass. Sevens can always be played. Put each seven in the middle to start a row.
3. **Extend the rows.** Once a seven is down, the eight of the same suit can go on one side and the six on the other. Add further cards in suit order: upward to the king, downward to the ace.
4. **Play or pass.** On your turn you must play if you can. If you cannot, you pass.
5. **Go out.** The first player to play their last card wins.

## After playing, talk about

- When did you choose a card that opened few plays for the others?
- Did you ever hold back a card on purpose? What did it do to the others?
- How would paying a chip for each pass change how people play?
