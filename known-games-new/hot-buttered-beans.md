# Hot Buttered Beans — find it with help from hot and cold

- From: Hot Buttered Beans, a hide and seek game for small children
- Players: 2–6 players
- Time: 10 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: Hoyle's Rules of Games, under 'Hot Buttered Beans'

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

One player leaves the room while the others hide a small object. The seeker comes back and searches. The others call out how near the seeker is: cold, warm, hot. Nobody wins or loses; everyone takes a turn at seeking.

## What it shows a designer

The only thing the others may say is how close the seeker is. It shows how a game with no winner can still have a clear aim, and how hints that tell you more as you get closer keep a search moving.

## What you need

- a small object, such as a coin, a walnut or a pen
- a room with many places to hide it
- a clock, if you want a time limit

## How to play

1. **Choose the object.** Agree on a small object to hide.
2. **Send one out.** One player leaves the room. The others hide the object in a good place.
3. **Call.** When it is hidden, everyone calls out: Hot buttered beans, please come to supper!
4. **Search.** The seeker comes back and looks. The others call out how close the seeker is: icy, very cold, cold, warm, warmer, hot, burning.
5. **Next seeker.** When the object is found, another player is sent out. Carry on until everyone has been the seeker.
6. **No winners.** Nobody wins or loses. If you want a limit, give each seeker ten minutes.

## After playing, talk about

- How did the hints change what the seeker did next?
- What happened when the hint givers disagreed?
- How could you add a winner to this game without ruining it?
