# Crash — find the hidden word by matching places

- From: Jotto, a word-guessing game, as changed for postal play
- Players: 2 players
- Time: 15 min
- Made by: Traditional word game; the rules are described by Robert Schmittberger
- Rules from: New Rules for Classic Games, p. 130
- This version: The book lets players play by post; here the two players sit together and fix the number of words in each round

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

Each player secretly picks a five-letter word. Both then try to guess the other's word. After each guess you are told only how many letters are in exactly the right place.

## What it shows a designer

Each reply carries a small clue, so the game is a conversation of questions and numbers. It shows how a rule that gives only partial feedback makes players build a picture from many small facts.

## What you need

- paper and pencils
- a dictionary, or a phone to settle whether a word is real

## How to play

1. **Choose a word.** Each player secretly writes a five-letter word that is in the dictionary. No names, plurals or short forms. Letters may repeat.
2. **Set the round sizes.** Agree how many guesses each round holds. A plan the book suggests is four in the first round, three in the second, two in the third, then one in every round after.
3. **Guess.** At the same time, both players write their guesses for the round and show them.
4. **Score.** For each guess, tell the other player how many letters are in the same place as in your secret word. HEART against SPADE scores 1, for the A; TRICK against TREAT scores 2, for the T and R.
5. **Win.** The first player to score 5 has found the word. If both find it in the same round, the player whose scoring word came earlier in the round wins. A wrong score, or a word not in the dictionary, loses the game.

## After playing, talk about

- Which guess taught you most, and what did you do with the answer?
- How did you choose a secret word that would be hard to find?
- Why did the book make a wrong score an instant loss?
