# Concentration — remember where the pairs are

- From: Concentration, a memory card game
- Players: 2–5 players
- Time: 10–15 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Everything Games Book, p. 67

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

All 52 cards lie face down in rows. On your turn you turn over two cards, hoping they match. If they do, you keep them and go again. If not, they go back face down, and everyone has now seen them.

## What it shows a designer

Nobody has a private hand, so the secret is the position of cards that were seen once. Every turn that fails gives information to the next player. It shows a game that runs on memory, not on cards or dice.

## What you need

- a deck of 52 cards

## How to play

1. **Lay out.** Shuffle and spread all the cards face down in neat rows.
2. **First turn.** Cut for who goes first. The first player turns over any two cards and shows them to everyone.
3. **Match.** If the two cards are the same number or the same picture, the player takes both and turns over two more.
4. **No match.** If they do not match, turn both back face down in the same places. Play passes to the player on the left.
5. **End.** When all the cards are gone, the player with the most matched pairs wins. The value of the cards does not matter.

## After playing, talk about

- How did you remember where cards were, and what did you do when you could not?
- Who gained most from a failed turn: the player who made it, or the next one?
- Does going first or going last help?
