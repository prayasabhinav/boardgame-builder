# Five-Card Stud — one card hidden, the rest on show

- From: Five-Card Stud, an old form of Poker
- Players: 2–10 players
- Time: 20–30 min
- Made by: Traditional; played in the United States
- Rules from: Hoyle's Rules of Games, under 'Five-Card Stud'
- This version: Five deals, a bet limit of 5 chips, and most chips at the end wins

Each player has one card hidden and four cards face up on the table. After every new card there is a round of betting. The face-up cards let everyone guess what the hidden one might be.

## What it shows a designer

Part of every hand is public. It shows how showing some information changes how people bet, because you can see what your rivals might have and they can see what you might have.

## What you need

- one pack of 52 playing cards
- about 30 chips, coins or dried beans for each player
- a sheet that lists the hand order, highest first: straight flush, four of a kind, full house, flush, straight, three of a kind, two pairs, one pair, highest card

## How to play

1. **Ante and deal.** Everyone puts one chip in the middle. Deal each player one card face down (the hole card) and one card face up. Players look at their own hole card but do not show it.
2. **First betting.** The player showing the highest card bets first and must bet. Everyone after calls, raises or drops out. Dropping out forfeits the chips you put in.
3. **More cards.** Deal one more face-up card to each player still in. The player whose face-up cards make the best poker combination bets first. A pair beats no pair; with no pair, the highest card leads. This time that player may check instead.
4. **Twice more.** Repeat so that each player has four face-up cards and one hole card, with betting after each card.
5. **Showdown.** After the last betting, players still in turn over the hole card. The best five-card hand takes the middle. Ties share it.
6. **Next deal.** The deal passes left. After five deals, the player with the most chips wins.

## After playing, talk about

- What could you work out about each rival from the cards they showed?
- When did you stay in because of your hole card alone?
- How did seeing most of the cards change when people dropped out?
