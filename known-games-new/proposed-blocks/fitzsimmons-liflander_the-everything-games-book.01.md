## Proposed block: jumping over pieces
- Slot: core-mechanic
- Id: jumping-over-pieces
- Why no block covers it: This block is already proposed from The Big Book of Rules (Checkers and Chinese Checkers). This book adds more evidence, not a second block.
- Games in this book that use it: Checkers, where a piece jumps an enemy piece to an empty square behind it, may take several in one move, and a player who skips a jump can be penalised by having the piece removed (pp. 4–5); Chinese Checkers, where a marble jumps any marble in a line of hops and nothing is removed (p. 10); the Knight in Chess, the only piece that jumps over others (p. 12).
- ? text: Your piece hops over another piece and lands in the empty space behind it. In checkers the piece you hop over is taken away; in Chinese Checkers it stays.
- Drawing: see the existing proposal for this block; no new drawing.

## Proposed block: scoring cards named in advance
- Slot: core-mechanic
- Id: scoring-cards
- Why no block covers it: Trick-taking rewards winning tricks, and it misses games where only a few named cards (the top trump, the bottom trump, the Jack, the Ten, the Five) carry the points.
- Games in this book that use it: All-Fours, with High, Low, Jack and Game (p. 36); Auction Pitch (p. 40); California Jack (p. 41); Pedro Sancho, where the Five of trumps is worth five points and the Nine nine (pp. 42–44).
- ? text: Winning tricks is only the way to reach the prizes. The prizes are named cards, such as the highest trump or the Jack of trumps, and a single card can win more than one.
