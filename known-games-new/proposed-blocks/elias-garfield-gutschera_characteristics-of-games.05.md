## Proposed block: handicap
- Slot: core-mechanic
- Id: handicap
- Why no block covers it: change the rules (nearest) lets players alter the game for everyone, but does not cover giving one player a head start or a penalty so unequal players meet evenly.
- Games in this book that use it, with pages: golf (points), go (stones), horse racing (weight), speed chess (different amounts of time); the book says handicaps fit low-luck games best (p. 230).
- ? text: A stronger player starts with less, such as fewer stones, fewer points or less time, so that a weaker player has a real chance. The two players start unequal on purpose.

## Proposed block: build your own side
- Slot: core-mechanic
- Id: build-your-own-side
- Why no block covers it: hand management is about playing cards from a hand during play, not about choosing a deck, character or army before play begins.
- Games in this book that use it, with pages: Magic: The Gathering decks, role-playing characters, Cosmic Encounter player abilities, Impossible Creatures armies (p. 229); character classes in shooters and races in strategy games (p. 227).
- ? text: Before or during play, each player picks the character, deck or army they will play. Two players can arrive with very different sides.
