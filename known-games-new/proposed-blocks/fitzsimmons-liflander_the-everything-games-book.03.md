## Proposed block: one against the rest
- Slot: player-interaction
- Id: one-against-the-rest
- Why no block covers it: Team vs team has two sides that are meant to be even, and direct confrontation has everyone against everyone; neither covers one player who chooses to play alone against all the others joined together.
- Games in this book that use it: Napoleon, where the highest bidder plays against everyone else combined (p. 105); Skat, where one lone player faces two opponents and any extra players share the opponents' result (p. 133); Solo, where a bidder may play alone against three others (pp. 145-148); Three-Handed Euchre, where the player who makes trump plays against the other two (p. 104).

## Proposed block: melding
- Slot: core-mechanic
- Id: melding
- Already proposed in morehead-mott-smith_hoyles-rules-of-games.02.md; this section only adds games from this book.
- Games in this book that use it: Rummy, where runs and lays are laid face up and cards are added to any player's set (pp. 128-129); Pinochle, where combinations such as a marriage or a pinochle are announced and laid face up after a trick (pp. 115-117). Gin Rummy keeps its sets in the hand (p. 130).

## Proposed block: taking the discard pile
- Slot: core-mechanic
- Id: taking-the-pile
- Already proposed in morehead-mott-smith_hoyles-rules-of-games.02.md; this section only adds games from this book.
- Games in this book that use it: Rummy, where a player may take the whole discard pile if the top card can be used in a set at once (p. 129). Gin Rummy takes only the top card (p. 130).

## Proposed block: wild cards
- Slot: core-mechanic
- Id: wild-cards
- Already proposed in morehead-mott-smith_hoyles-rules-of-games.02.md; this section only adds games from this book.
- Games in this book that use it: Mistigris, where the joker can be called any card not already in the hand (p. 125). Railroad Euchre adds a joker that is always trump, which is a top card and not a wild one (p. 104).
