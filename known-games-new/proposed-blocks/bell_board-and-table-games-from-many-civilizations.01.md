## Proposed block: special squares
- Slot: core-mechanic
- Id: special-squares
- Why no block covers it: Roll and move sends a piece along a path by the dice, and it misses spaces on the path that carry their own rule, such as a safe square or a trap.
- Games in this book that use it: Pachisi castles (pp. 9, 11), Palm Tree Game gold points (p. 21), Royal Game of Goose bridge, well and prison (p. 16), Thaayam palace squares (pp. 18-19), Sumerian game marked squares (p. 25), Thirty Squares (p. 28).
- ? text: Some spaces on the track have a rule of their own. One may keep you safe, another may send you back or make you miss a turn.

## Proposed block: landing sends a piece back
- Slot: core-mechanic
- Id: landing-sends-back
- Why no block covers it: Direct confrontation (a player interaction block) says players attack each other, but no core mechanic covers a piece that lands on another and sends it back to the start.
- Games in this book that use it: Nyout (p. 2), Zohn Ahl (p. 5), Pachisi (p. 11), Thaayam (p. 19), Tabula (p. 35), Backgammon (p. 41).
- ? text: If your piece lands on a space where another player's piece stands, theirs goes back to the start. Some safe spaces protect a piece from this.

## Proposed block: extra turn
- Slot: timing
- Id: extra-turn
- Why no block covers it: Take turns says one player acts at a time in order, and it misses rules that give the same player another go after a special throw or a hit.
- Games in this book that use it: Nyout (p. 2), Zohn Ahl (p. 5), Pachisi (p. 11), Thaayam (pp. 18-19), Palm Tree Game (p. 21), Sumerian game (p. 24), Chasing the Girls (p. 37).
- ? text: On some throws, or when you hit another piece, you get another go at once. A lucky player can move many times before anyone else.
