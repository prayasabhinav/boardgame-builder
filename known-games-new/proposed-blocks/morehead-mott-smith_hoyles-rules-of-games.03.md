## Proposed block: spare hand (widow)
- Slot: core-mechanic
- Id: spare-hand
- Why no block covers it: Hand management is about choosing cards from the hand you hold, and it misses an extra hand, dealt face down, that one player may take or buy.
- Games in this book that use it, under their headings: Michigan (the widow is sold at auction), Five Hundred, Rauber Skat (the skat), Schafkopf, Six-bid Solo, Hasenpfeffer, Auction Pinochle (the widow, buried cards).
- ? text: An extra hand is dealt face down in the middle. One player gets to take it, swap it or buy it, then puts cards back before play begins.

## Proposed block: declaring combinations
- Slot: core-mechanic
- Id: declaring-combinations
- Why no block covers it: Set collection scores groups gathered by the end, and melding lays sets down to add to; neither covers showing a pair or run in the hand mid-game for points while play goes on.
- Games in this book that use it, under their headings: Sixty-Six (a king and queen of one suit, "marriage"), Klaberjass (runs of three or more, and king and queen of trumps), Two-Hand Pinochle, Auction Pinochle and Partnership Auction Pinochle (marriages, runs, four of a kind).
- ? text: You show a pair or a run you hold, such as a king and queen of one suit, and score points for it while the game goes on. The cards stay in your hand.
