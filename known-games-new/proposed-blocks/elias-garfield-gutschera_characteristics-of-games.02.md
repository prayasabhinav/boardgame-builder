## Proposed block: choose your target
- Slot: core-mechanic
- Id: choose-your-target
- Why no block covers it: direct confrontation (a player interaction block) says players attack each other, but no core mechanic covers the act of picking which rival is hit.
- Games in this book that use it, with pages: the chip-taking game (p. 48); Truel, where each shot goes at a rival of your choice (pp. 50-51); the balloon game (p. 55); Risk, where you may only pick neighbours (p. 60); Quake and Assassin, where range or an assigned victim narrows the choice (pp. 60, 60 note 25). The book treats this as a general kind of rule, called targeted interaction (p. 46).
- ? text: On your turn you pick which player is hit, helped or traded with. Who you choose matters as much as what you do. Three or more players make this a hard choice.
