## Proposed block: capture and rescue
- Slot: core-mechanic
- Id: capture-and-rescue
- Why no block covers it: Area control (nearest) is about holding spots, and it misses games where a caught player is held in a jail and a teammate can free them by reaching the jail.
- Games in this book that use it: Capture the Flag, where prisoners are freed by a touch (p. 174); Prisoner's Base (p. 174); Hide and Seek, where captured hiders are freed with the call alley, alley, oxen free (p. 164); Kick the Can, where kicking the can frees all prisoners (p. 183); Ringoleavio, where touching the base frees them (p. 190).
- ? text: If you are caught, you wait in a jail. A teammate who reaches the jail without being caught can set you free.

## Proposed block: passing a message along
- Slot: core-mechanic
- Id: passing-a-message
- Why no block covers it: Call and response (nearest) is a call answered by a move, and it misses games where one message travels through every player in turn and may change on the way.
- Games in this book that use it: Telephone, where a whispered phrase goes round the circle and the last player says it aloud (p. 154); Western Union and Electricity, where a hand squeeze travels round a circle of players (p. 155).
- ? text: A message travels from player to player, by whisper or by squeeze. It may change on the way, and nobody can check it until it arrives.
