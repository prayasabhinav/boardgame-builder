## Proposed block: trap between two
- Slot: core-mechanic
- Id: trap-between-two
- Why no block covers it: area control (nearest) is about holding places, and misses taking a piece by standing on both sides of it.
- Games in this book that use it, with pages: Seega (pp. 82–83), Ludus Latrunculorum (p. 86), Hasami Shogi (p. 97), Tablut (p. 76), and the Tafl game with rook-moving pieces whose rules are listed on p. 81.
- ? text: You take an enemy piece by standing on both sides of it in a straight line. A piece can walk between two enemies safely, but it cannot stay there if you close in.

- Id: make-a-row-take-a-piece

## Proposed block: betting on a throw
- Slot: core-mechanic
- Id: betting-on-a-throw
- Why no block covers it: bidding (nearest) is about offering money to buy something, and misses putting money on what a coin or dice will show.
- Games in this book that use it, with pages: Heads and Tails (p. 125), Barbudi, where players bet on the shooter or the fader (pp. 128–129), Hazard (pp. 130–131).
- ? text: Players put money or things on the table before the dice or coin land, and the throw decides who takes them. The skill is in choosing the bet, not in the throw.
