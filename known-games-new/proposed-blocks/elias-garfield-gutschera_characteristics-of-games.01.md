## Proposed block: best of several games
- Slot: victory-condition
- Id: best-of-several-games
- Why no block covers it: most points (nearest) decides a winner at the end of one game, but does not cover a winner chosen by who takes the most of a fixed run of whole games.
- Games in this book that use it: chess and go matches (pp. 13-14), tennis matches (p. 13), rubber bridge (p. 17); the book calls a match a fixed run of games, often best two out of three, ended as soon as one side cannot be caught.
- ? text: Play several short games in a row. Whoever wins the most games wins, and you can stop as soon as nobody can catch up. Best two out of three is the simplest kind.
