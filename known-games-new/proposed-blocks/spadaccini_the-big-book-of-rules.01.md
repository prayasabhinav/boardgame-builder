## Proposed block: jumping over pieces
- Slot: core-mechanic
- Id: jumping-over-pieces
- Why no block covers it: Area control is about holding spots, and it misses moves where a piece hops over another piece to land beyond it.
- Games in this book that use it: Checkers, where a piece jumps over an enemy piece and removes it (p. 9); Chinese Checkers, where a marble hops over any marble and nothing is removed (p. 22); Turkish Checkers and International Checkers (p. 11).
- ? text: Your piece hops over another piece and lands in the empty space behind it. In checkers the piece you hop over is taken away; in Chinese Checkers it stays.

## Proposed block: sowing seeds
- Slot: core-mechanic
- Id: sowing-seeds
- Why no block covers it: Roll and move sends one piece along a path, and the nearest block misses picking up a handful and dropping one seed in each pit in turn.
- Games in this book that use it: Wari, an African seed-sowing game (p. 38), and its variation Grand Slam (p. 39). The book says the family has more than a hundred variations.
- ? text: You scoop up all the seeds from one pit and drop them one at a time into the pits that follow. Where your last seed lands decides what you can take.

## Proposed block: yes-or-no questions
- Slot: core-mechanic
- Id: yes-or-no-questions
- Why no block covers it: Call and response lets either player say anything in reply, and it misses a game built on asking questions that only allow yes or no.
- Games in this book that use it: Twenty Questions (p. 84), Botticelli (p. 45), Coffeepot (p. 69).
- ? text: One player knows a secret. The others ask questions that can be answered yes or no, and each answer shrinks the list of things it could be.

## Proposed block: a list that keeps growing
- Slot: core-mechanic
- Id: growing-list
- Why no block covers it: Hand management asks you to remember cards in your hand, and it misses a game where every player must repeat a shared list that gets longer each turn.
- Games in this book that use it: I Went to the Market and its variations, The Alphabet Game and I Packed My Bag (p. 75–76); Concentration played with dominoes, which also asks for memory of face-down tiles (p. 30).
- ? text: Each player says the whole list so far and adds one new item. The list grows on every turn, so remembering it is the game.
