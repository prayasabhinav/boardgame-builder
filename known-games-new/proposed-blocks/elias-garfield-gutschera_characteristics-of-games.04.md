## Proposed block: secret scores
- Slot: information
- Id: secret-scores
- Why no block covers it: hidden hands (nearest) covers cards only their holder sees, but not points or money that a player earns and keeps secret so rivals cannot add up the totals.
- Games in this book that use it, with pages: European boardgames with a hidden victory-point chit drawn by each player (p. 164); boardgames where a player's gold or money is kept secret, partly by writing some of it on a card nobody else sees (pp. 201-202). The book describes this as a general trick for removing the dull job of counting everyone's total before a key move.
- ? text: Some of your points or money are kept secret from the other players. Nobody can add up the scores for certain, so everyone has to guess who is ahead.
