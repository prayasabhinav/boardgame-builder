# Footbag circle — keep it in the air together

- From: Hacky Sack (footbag)
- Players: 3–8 players
- Time: 5–15 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: Characteristics of Games, p. 294
- This version: Use a small cloth bag filled with rice or beans, and count your best run together.

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

Players stand in a circle and kick a small beanbag up into the air, again and again, so it never touches the ground.

## What it shows a designer

Nobody wins and nobody loses to anyone else. It shows a game where the only aim is to keep it going, and where a count, if players want one, is their own idea.

## What you need

- a small beanbag, or a cloth bag filled with rice
- flat open ground

## How to play

1. **Circle.** Stand in a circle, about two steps apart.
2. **Start.** One player drops the bag and kicks it up.
3. **Pass it on.** Anyone in the circle kicks it up again, using their feet only. Pass it to a different player each time if you can.
4. **Drop.** If the bag touches the ground, it starts again.
5. **Count.** If you want, count kicks in a run and try to beat your last one. The book gives no scoring rule.

## After playing, talk about

- What did you do to help a weaker player keep the bag up?
- When did the group stop talking and just focus?
- What would you add if you wanted someone to win?
