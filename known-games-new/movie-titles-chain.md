# Movie Titles Chain — start with the last letter

- From: Movie Titles, a spoken word-chain game that also works with songs and TV shows
- Players: 2 or more players
- Time: 10 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Big Book of Rules, p. 125
- This version: Titles can be films, songs or serials in any language the class shares; the opening word The does not count

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

One player names a film. The next player names a film whose title begins with the last letter of that title, and so on. A player who takes too long is out.

## What it shows a designer

A chain game shows how one shared rule (the last letter) and a clock can keep a simple game moving. It also shows how every reply makes the next harder, because the letters left are fewer.

## What you need

- players who know many film titles
- a watch or phone for the ten-second limit

## How to play

1. **Start.** One player names any film.
2. **Chain.** The next player names a film whose title starts with the last letter of the title just named. The opening word The does not count.
3. **Time limit.** A player has ten seconds to answer. If they cannot, they are out.
4. **Repeat.** Titles cannot be used twice in one game.
5. **Win.** Play goes round until one player is left. That player wins.

## After playing, talk about

- Which letters ended up hardest to follow?
- How did the ten-second limit change the titles you picked?
- What changes if the game is played in teams?
