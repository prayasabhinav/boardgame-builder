# Rounce — play, pass, or take the dummy hand

- From: Rounce, a trick-taking game
- Players: 3–7 players
- Time: 15–20 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Everything Games Book, pp. 125–126
- This version: Without the book's penalties for dealing and talking mistakes

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

Everyone starts with 15 points and wants to reach zero. Each trick you take removes one point. If you play and take no trick at all, you are rounced and get five points added.

## What it shows a designer

You choose before play whether to join in, and a spare hand is waiting on the table if you dislike your own. It shows how a choice at the start lowers the cost of a bad deal.

## What you need

- a standard deck of 52 cards
- paper and a pencil to keep score

## How to play

1. **Deal.** Deal five cards to each player, two then three at a time. Deal a spare hand of six cards face down in the middle: the dummy. Turn up the next card. Its suit is trump.
2. **Choose.** Starting on the dealer's left, each player looks at their hand and says "I play," "I pass," or takes the dummy. Taking the dummy means putting your own hand face down in the middle and discarding one card from the dummy, so you keep five.
3. **Dealer's swap.** If the dealer plays their own hand, they may swap one card from it for the turned-up trump card.
4. **Everyone refuses.** If everyone refuses, the player on the dealer's right must play or take the dummy, or the dealer is given five points.
5. **Play.** The player on the dealer's left leads. Each player who is playing follows suit if they can, and may trump if they cannot. The highest card of the suit led wins, but trump beats it.
6. **Lead trump.** The winner of a trick must lead a trump if they hold one. Otherwise they lead any card.
7. **Scores.** Everyone starts on 15. Each trick you win takes one point off your score. If you chose to play and won no tricks, add five points. The first player to reach zero wins.

## After playing, talk about

- When was it right to pass, and when to take the dummy?
- How did the winner leading trump change which cards you kept?
- What would happen if there were no penalty for taking no trick?
