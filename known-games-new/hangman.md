# Hangman — guess the word before the drawing is finished

- From: Hangman, a pencil-and-paper word game
- Players: 2 players
- Time: 5–10 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Everything Games Book, p. 286

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

One player thinks of a word and draws a blank for each letter. The other guesses letters. A right guess fills in the blanks. A wrong guess adds a part to a drawing of a hanged figure. The guesser must find the word before the drawing is whole.

## What it shows a designer

A game can be a race between two things on the same page: the word filling in and the drawing growing. It also shows how a hidden answer, plus a cost for every wrong guess, makes each guess a small risk.

## What you need

- a sheet of paper
- a pencil or pen

## How to play

1. **Choose a word.** The first player thinks of a real word that the other player knows how to spell. Keep it secret.
2. **Draw the blanks.** Draw one short line for each letter of the word. Then draw a simple gallows with a noose.
3. **Guess a letter.** The other player names a letter. If it is in the word, write it on every blank where it belongs.
4. **Wrong guesses.** If the letter is not in the word, write it to one side. Then add one part to the hanged figure, starting with a circle for the head.
5. **Finish.** The guesser wins by naming the whole word before the figure is complete. If the figure is complete first, the word-setter wins.
6. **Swap.** After a win by the guesser, the guesser chooses the next word.

## After playing, talk about

- Which letters did you guess first, and why?
- How did the growing drawing change the risks you took?
- What could you change so the word-setter has choices during the game?
