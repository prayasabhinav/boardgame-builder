# Button, Button — warmer, colder, hot

- From: Button, Button, Who's Got the Button?, a hide-and-find game
- Players: 2 or more players
- Time: 10 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Big Book of Rules, p. 160

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

One player hides a button. The others search. The hider can only say warmer, colder, hot or freezing.

## What it shows a designer

The hider knows exactly where the button is but is allowed to give only a little help. It shows how a limited word, not a map, can guide a search.

## What you need

- a button or coin
- a room with places to hide it

## How to play

1. **Hide.** One player hides a button while the others wait outside or look away.
2. **Search.** The others come in and start to look.
3. **Hints.** The hider says warmer if a seeker is moving closer, colder if moving away, hot when very close, freezing when very far.
4. **Find.** The aim of the seekers is to find the button.
5. **Play again.** If you play another round, agree before you start who hides next; the book does not say.

## After playing, talk about

- Which hints helped most?
- How did you decide where to look first?
- What other single words could the hider use?
