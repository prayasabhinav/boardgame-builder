# Mother, May I? — ask first, and she decides

- From: Mother, May I?, a children's party game
- Players: 4–12 players
- Time: 10–15 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Everything Games Book, p. 277
- This version: Played across a classroom or corridor with a start line and a finish line about ten steps apart, in place of fifty yards

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

One player is the Mother and stands at the far end. The others line up at the start and ask to move. The Mother says yes or no to each request. The first player to tag the Mother becomes the next Mother.

## What it shows a designer

One player can run the whole game just by saying yes or no. It shows how a single judge, and a polite phrase players must remember, can slow some players and speed others.

## What you need

- a start line and a finish line, drawn with chalk or marked with tape
- one player to be the Mother

## How to play

1. **Set up.** The Mother stands at the far end of the space, facing the line of players.
2. **Call a name.** The Mother names a player and gives an order, such as 'Take three giant steps forward'.
3. **Ask.** The player must answer 'Mother, may I?' before moving. The Mother answers 'Yes, you may' or 'No, you may not'.
4. **Move or stay.** On a yes, the player does the order. On a no, the player stays where they are. The Mother then names another player.
5. **Keep track.** The Mother has to remember where every player stands. A Mother who loses track of the players is likely to be caught out.
6. **Win.** The first player to reach the Mother and tag her wins the round and becomes the next Mother.

## After playing, talk about

- How did the Mother choose between yes and no, and did she treat players differently?
- What happened when someone forgot to ask?
- What would change if two Mothers shared the job?
