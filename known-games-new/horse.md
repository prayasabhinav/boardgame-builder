# HORSE — match the shot or earn a letter

- From: HORSE, a basketball shooting game
- Players: 2 or more players
- Time: 10–15 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: Characteristics of Games, p. 294
- This version: Toss a paper ball into a bin instead of using a basketball and hoop. The book gives only the outline, so the order of turns is chosen here, and the last player left wins.

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

Players try to match difficult shots made by the others. Each time you miss a shot you must match, you get the next letter of the word HORSE. When you have spelt the whole word, you are out.

## What it shows a designer

The players write the challenges themselves. It shows how a game can grow harder by itself when each player sets a task for the next.

## What you need

- a bin or a bucket
- a few sheets of scrap paper rolled into balls
- a marked line on the floor to throw from

## How to play

1. **Order.** Choose who goes first. Turns go round the group.
2. **Set a shot.** On your turn, make a difficult shot for the others to match.
3. **Match it.** If you make the shot, the next player must make the same shot in the same way. If you miss, the next player sets a shot instead.
4. **Letters.** A player who misses a shot they had to match gets one letter of HORSE, in order.
5. **Out.** A player who has all five letters is out of the game.
6. **Win.** Play goes on until all but one player have spelled the word. This version counts the last player left as the winner.

## After playing, talk about

- How hard did you make the shots you set, and why?
- Did you ever set a shot to beat one particular player?
- What would change if everyone got ten letters?
