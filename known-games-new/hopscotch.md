# Hopscotch — hop the boxes, kicking the stone ahead

- From: Hopscotch, a children's game played in many countries
- Players: 2–6 players
- Time: 10–15 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Everything Games Book, p. 252
- This version: An eight-box court, with the rules about faltering restated simply; the book's picture of the court is not copied

A court of numbered boxes is drawn on the ground. Each player tosses a stone into box 1, hops through the boxes, kicks the stone out and tosses it into the next box. The first to finish the whole court without a slip wins.

## What it shows a designer

A race can be run on a path you draw yourself. It shows how a course that asks for different movements in different places makes the same path harder in some boxes than others.

## What you need

- chalk, or tape, to draw the court on a hard floor or pavement
- one small stone or coin for each player

## How to play

1. **Draw the court.** Draw eight boxes. Boxes 1, 2 and 3 are in a line. Boxes 4 and 5 sit side by side. Box 6 is alone. Boxes 7 and 8 sit side by side. Draw a starting line a step or two before box 1.
2. **Toss.** From the starting line, toss your stone into box 1.
3. **Hop.** Hop on one foot into each single box, and straddle the two boxes of a pair, with one foot in each. Do not touch a line. Do not put down your raised foot.
4. **Kick.** Hop up to the box with your stone and kick the stone out of the court. Then toss it into the next numbered box and go again.
5. **Slip.** Your turn ends if the stone lands in the wrong box, if you kick it into the wrong place, if you step on a line or if your raised foot touches the ground. The next player goes. When your turn comes again, you try the same box once more.
6. **Win.** The first player to complete the court without a slip wins. Players may agree to change the number of boxes.

## After playing, talk about

- Which box was the hardest, and what made it hard?
- How did you plan your hops before you started?
- What would change if the court had a different shape?
