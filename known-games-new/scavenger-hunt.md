# Scavenger Hunt — bring back the list

- From: Scavenger Hunt, a team search game
- Players: 6 or more players, in teams of 3 or 4
- Time: 30 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Big Book of Rules, p. 128
- This version: Items found on the school grounds or the classroom floor, not by knocking on neighbours' doors

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

Every team gets the same list of things to find. The first team back with everything wins. A team that returns late is out.

## What it shows a designer

Because every team has the same list, the contest is about how teams split the work and how quickly they decide. It shows a race where everyone has the same goal but different routes.

## What you need

- a list of items for each team, the same on every list
- a bag for each team
- a watch

## How to play

1. **Make the list.** Write the same list of ten items for each team. Choose items that can be found at school, such as a feather, a pencil shorter than two inches, a blue pen, a leaf with three points.
2. **Set the time.** Agree on a limit, such as twenty minutes.
3. **Search.** Teams go off to find the items and put them in their bag.
4. **Return.** A team that comes back after the time limit is out.
5. **Win.** The first team back with every item wins.

## After playing, talk about

- How did your team split the list?
- Which item was hardest to find?
- What would change if the lists were different for each team?

- Core mechanic: set collection; hide and find
