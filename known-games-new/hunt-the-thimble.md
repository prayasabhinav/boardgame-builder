# Hunt the Thimble — find the hidden thing by warm and cold

- From: Hunt the Thimble, a traditional party game
- Players: 3–10 players
- Time: 10–15 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Everything Games Book, p. 251
- This version: A coin or button stands in for the thimble, and the hunt takes place in one room

One player hides a small object while the others are out of sight. The others then hunt for it. The hider may call out 'warm', 'warmer', 'hot' or 'cold' to say how near they are, but may never mislead them.

## What it shows a designer

A game can be built on one player who knows the answer and may only give a hint. It shows how the kind of hint decides how long the hunt lasts.

## What you need

- a coin, a button or a thimble
- a room with places to hide something small

## How to play

1. **Show the object.** Everyone sees the object. One player is chosen to be the hider.
2. **Hide it.** The others cover their eyes or leave the room. The hider puts the object somewhere in the room. It may be in an odd place where it can be seen, or out of sight.
3. **Hunt.** At the signal, everyone starts to hunt.
4. **Give hints.** The hider may say 'warm' as players come near, 'warmer' as they get closer and 'hot' when they are very near. If players move away, the hider says 'cool' or 'cold'. The hider may also say nothing. The hider may not give a false hint.
5. **Find it.** The first player to find the object is the winner of the round and hides it in the next game.

## After playing, talk about

- Which hints told you most, and which told you least?
- How did your search change once you heard 'cold'?
- What would change if the hider were allowed to give a false hint?
