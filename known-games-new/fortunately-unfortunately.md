# Fortunately, Unfortunately — lucky news, unlucky news

- From: Fortunately, Unfortunately, a travel word game
- Players: 2 or more players
- Time: 10 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Big Book of Rules, p. 207

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

Players build a story together. One starts a sentence with Fortunately, the next goes on with Unfortunately, and so on, until someone cannot think of one.

## What it shows a designer

The story swings between luck and mischance, so each player sets up the next. It shows how a loose rule (alternate two words) can drive a whole story with no plan.

## What you need

- a group of players, no equipment

## How to play

1. **Start.** The first player says a sentence that begins with Fortunately, such as Fortunately, I am hungry and the shop is two streets away.
2. **Reply.** The next player says a sentence that follows from it and begins with Unfortunately, such as Unfortunately, the shop only sells onions.
3. **Alternate.** The third player starts with Fortunately again, and each sentence has to follow from the one before.
4. **Stumped.** Play goes on round the group until a player cannot think of a sentence.
5. **Next round.** The book does not say who starts the next story, so agree that before you start.

## After playing, talk about

- Which turn was hardest to answer, and why?
- How did the story's direction change from one sentence to the next?
- What would you add to give the group something to aim at?
