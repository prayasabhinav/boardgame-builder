# Draw Poker Dice — keep some dice, throw the rest

- From: Draw Poker, played with dice
- Players: Several players
- Time: 10 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Everything Games Book, p. 182
- This version: The best hand in each round scores a point, and the first to three points wins

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

Each player throws five dice, then may pick up any of them and throw those again once. Hands are ranked like poker hands, from one pair up to five dice alike.

## What it shows a designer

One extra throw lets a player mend a bad start. It shows how a rule that lets you keep part of a result and risk the rest gives luck a shape the player can steer.

## What you need

- five dice
- paper and a pen to keep the score

## How to play

1. **First throw.** In turn, each player throws all five dice.
2. **Second throw.** The player may leave all five on the table, or pick up as many as they like and throw those again once. The dice they keep stay where they are.
3. **Ranking.** From lowest to highest: one pair, two pairs, three of a kind, full house (three of one number and two of another), four of a kind, five alike. Among equal kinds, the higher number of spots wins. Six is high and one is low. Five sixes cannot be beaten.
4. **Round winner.** When everyone has played, the best hand scores one point. If two hands tie, those players throw once more, all five dice.
5. **Win.** The first player to three points wins.

## After playing, talk about

- Which dice did you keep, and which did you throw again?
- When did you throw a good pair again, hoping for more?
- How much did the second throw change who won?
