# Free Association — say the first word that comes

- From: Free Association (Word Association), a travel word game
- Players: 2 or more players
- Time: 5 to 10 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Big Book of Rules, p. 207

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

One player says a word. The next says the first word that comes to mind, a related word or one that rhymes. Anyone who hesitates is out.

## What it shows a designer

Speed is the only rule. It shows how a clock made of other people's patience can do the work of a rule.

## What you need

- a group of players, no equipment
- an agreed count (about three seconds) for hesitation

## How to play

1. **Start.** One player says any word.
2. **Answer.** The next player says the first word that comes to mind: a word linked to it in meaning, or one that rhymes.
3. **Chain.** Each player answers the word just said, so the chain wanders, as in plumber, pipe, smoke, fire, wire.
4. **Out.** A player who pauses longer than the agreed count is out.
5. **Win.** The last player left wins.

## After playing, talk about

- Where did the chain drift furthest from where it began?
- Did your answers come from meaning or from sound?
- How would a shorter count change the game?
