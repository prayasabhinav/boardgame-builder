# Telephone — pass the whisper along

- From: Telephone (Chinese Whispers, Gossip), a whispering game
- Players: 6 or more players
- Time: 5 to 10 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Big Book of Rules, p. 154

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

Players sit in a line or circle. The first thinks of a message of at least ten words and whispers it to the next player. The message goes all the way round and the last player says it out loud.

## What it shows a designer

Nobody wins or loses. It shows what happens to a message when each person has to pass it by ear only, and how small changes pile up.

## What you need

- a group of players sitting side by side

## How to play

1. **Think.** The first player thinks of a message of at least ten words and tells no one.
2. **Whisper.** The first player whispers it once to the player next to them. No repeating.
3. **Pass.** Each player whispers what they heard to the next player.
4. **Say it.** The last player says the message aloud, and everyone hears how it has changed.
5. **Try two.** For a second round, send two different messages round at once, one to the right and one to the left.

## After playing, talk about

- Where did the message change most, and why?
- What kind of messages stay the same, and which fall apart?
- What would you do to make a message travel without losing anything?

## Its blocks

- Core mechanic: call and response; passing a message along
- Victory condition: no objective
- Player interaction: solo in parallel
- Chance or skill: mostly chance
- Timing: take turns
