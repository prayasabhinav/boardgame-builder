# Indian Poker — bet on a card only the others can see

- From: Indian Poker, a small poker game
- Players: 3–8 players
- Time: 10–15 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Big Book of Rules, p. 311
- This version: Beans or coins for chips, five deals, and the player with the most chips at the end wins

Each player holds one card against their forehead, facing out. You see everyone's card except your own. You bet on whether yours is the highest.

## What it shows a designer

The usual secret is turned around: everyone knows your card but you. It shows how a game can make you work out your own position from the faces and bets of others.

## What you need

- a deck of 52 playing cards
- about 15 beans or coins for each player

## How to play

1. **Ante.** Everyone puts one chip in the pot.
2. **Deal.** Give each player one card. Without looking, each holds it against their forehead so the others can see it.
3. **Bet.** Starting left of the dealer, each player bets or folds. After a bet, others fold, call or raise in turn.
4. **Show.** Players still in lower their cards. The highest card takes the pot.
5. **Play on.** Play five deals. The player with the most chips wins.

## After playing, talk about

- What did you read from the other players' faces and bets?
- When did you fold, even though you could not see why?
- How is this different from a game where you know your own card?
