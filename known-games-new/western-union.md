# Western Union — a squeeze sent round the circle

- From: Western Union, a hand-squeezing circle game
- Players: 6 or more players
- Time: 10 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Big Book of Rules, p. 155

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

Players hold hands in a circle round one player, It, who covers their eyes. A telegram is sent by squeezing hands from player to player. It opens their eyes and tries to see who is passing it.

## What it shows a designer

The message travels by touch only, and the watcher has to spot a movement too small to hear. It shows how a game can be played without a single word.

## What you need

- a circle of players, no equipment

## How to play

1. **Set up.** One player is It and stands in the middle with eyes covered. The rest hold hands in a circle.
2. **Choose a recipient.** One player in the circle says, I am sending a telegram to, and names another player in the circle.
3. **Send.** The sender squeezes the hand of the player beside them. That player squeezes the next hand, and so on. The direction can change at any time.
4. **Watch.** It opens their eyes and looks for a squeeze moving round the circle.
5. **Caught.** If It spots someone squeezing, that player becomes It. If the recipient gets the squeeze first, they say so and It stays It for the next telegram.

## After playing, talk about

- How did you hide the squeeze?
- What made it harder to spot the sender?
- How would you pass a message in silence without touching?

## Its blocks

- Core mechanic: playing in silence; passing a message along
- Victory condition: one loser
- Chance or skill: mostly skill
- Information visibility: hidden identity
- Timing: real-time span
