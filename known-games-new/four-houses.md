# Four Houses — trade to build

- From: Catan, a trading board game by Klaus Teuber
- Players: 3–4 players
- Time: 15 min
- Made by: Made for this tool, after the trading in Catan

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

Each player produces only one kind of material, but a house needs one of every kind. Nobody can build without trading, and every trade helps someone else build too.

## What it shows a designer

When nobody can win alone, every deal is both help and risk. It shows how negotiation makes the other players the board you play on.

## What you need

- 40 paper slips: 10 each marked wood, brick, grain and wool (with 3 players, use only three kinds)
- a sheet each to draw your houses on

## How to play

1. **Take a kind.** Each player takes all 10 slips of one kind. Keep 3 in your hand and put the other 7 in front of you as your store.
2. **Produce.** On your turn, move 2 slips from your store into your hand. Keep your hand hidden.
3. **Trade.** Anyone can trade with anyone at any time, at any rate they agree. Promises are allowed, and nobody has to keep them.
4. **Build.** On your turn, pay one slip of every kind in play to draw a house. Each slip you pay goes back to the store of the player whose kind it is.
5. **Win.** The first player with 3 houses wins.

## After playing, talk about

- Whom did you trade with most, and why?
- When did you refuse a trade that would have helped you?
- Did a promise change how the game went?
