# Who Am I? — find out who is on your back

- From: Who Am I?, a party icebreaker
- Players: 6 or more players
- Time: 15 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Big Book of Rules, p. 132

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

Each guest wears the name of a famous person on their back where they cannot see it. They ask other guests questions that can only be answered yes or no, until they work out who they are.

## What it shows a designer

Here the secret is held by everyone except the person it is about. It shows how a game can reverse the usual hidden role: you are the only one who does not know.

## What you need

- name tags or sticky notes
- a pen
- pins or tape
- a host who writes the names and checks guesses

## How to play

1. **Make the tags.** The host writes the name of a famous person on each tag. Real or story characters both work. Use people the whole class knows.
2. **Stick them on.** As players arrive, the host sticks a tag on each player's back without letting them see it.
3. **Ask.** Players walk round and ask others questions that can be answered yes or no, such as are you alive, are you a woman, are you in films.
4. **Guess.** When you think you know, tell the host your guess. If it is right, take the tag off. If not, keep asking.
5. **End.** Play until everyone has found out, or stop at a time you set. The first to guess correctly can be named the winner.

## After playing, talk about

- Which questions narrowed the list fastest?
- How did it feel to be the only one who did not know?
- What would change if players could lie?

## Its blocks

- Core mechanic: call and response; yes-or-no questions
- Victory condition: race to finish
- Player interaction: solo in parallel
- Chance or skill: mostly skill
- Information visibility: hidden identity
- Timing: no turns
