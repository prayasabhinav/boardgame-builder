# Antakshari — answer with the last letter

- From: Antakshari, a song game played across India
- Players: 4–20 players in two teams
- Time: 10–20 min
- Made by: Traditional; played across India
- This version: A count of ten for each answer, and a point to the other team for every miss

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

One team sings a few lines of a song. The other team has to start a song with the sound the first team ended on. The game goes back and forth until one team runs dry.

## What it shows a designer

Each answer becomes the next challenge. It shows how call and response turns a game into a chain, where the move you make decides what the other side has to answer.

## What you need

- nothing but the players' voices
- someone to keep score on paper

## How to play

1. **Make two teams.** Split into two teams and sit facing each other.
2. **Open.** The first team sings a few lines of any song.
3. **The letter.** The last sound of the last word they sang is the letter for the other team.
4. **Answer.** The other team counts to ten out loud while they think, and must start singing a song that begins with that letter before ten. A song already sung cannot be used again.
5. **Miss.** If they cannot, the singing team scores a point, and the team that missed opens again with any song.
6. **End.** Play until one team has 10 points, or for 15 minutes. The team with more points wins.

## After playing, talk about

- Did a team end a song on purpose to leave a hard letter?
- How did the count of ten change who in a team spoke up?
- Which letters were hardest, and why?
