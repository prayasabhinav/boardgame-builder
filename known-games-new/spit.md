# Spit — two players race to empty their cards

- From: Spit, a very fast two-player card game
- Players: 2 players
- Time: 5–10 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Big Book of Rules, p. 339
- This version: One round, the first to clear their stock wins

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

Both players play at the same time, with no turns. Each tries to put cards in order, up or down, onto two piles in the middle before the other does.

## What it shows a designer

Nothing stops either player from moving, so the game is decided by looking and reaching faster than the other. It shows what happens to a card game when you remove turns completely.

## What you need

- a deck of 52 playing cards

## How to play

1. **Deal.** Deal all the cards, 26 each. Each player makes a face-down stock.
2. **Spreads.** Each player turns four cards face up in front of them. These are your spread.
3. **Spit.** Together count 'One, two, three, spit!' Each player turns the top card of their stock into the middle. This makes two spit piles.
4. **Race.** Both play at once, using one hand and one card at a time. A spread card or stock card goes on a spit pile if it is one higher or one lower than the pile's top card. A six goes on a five or a seven. Suit does not matter.
5. **Refill.** When a spread card leaves, fill the gap from your stock.
6. **Stuck.** When neither player can move, both turn a new card from the stock onto the spit piles and race again.
7. **Win.** The first player whose stock and spread are empty wins.

## After playing, talk about

- What did you look at first when a new card came down?
- When did speed matter more than planning?
- How would the game feel with turns?
