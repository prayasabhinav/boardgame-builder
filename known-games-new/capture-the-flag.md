# Capture the Flag — take their flag home

- From: Capture the Flag (Freedom, Drapeau), a team game said to be over 500 years old
- Players: 8 or more players, in two teams
- Time: 20 to 30 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Big Book of Rules, p. 173
- This version: A school ground or field, with a time limit of 20 minutes and cloth flags tied to sticks

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

Each team hides a flag on its own half of the ground. Players cross the line to take the other team's flag. A player tagged on the wrong side goes to jail and can be freed by a teammate.

## What it shows a designer

Two ways to win (flag or prisoners) give each player a choice between defending, raiding and rescuing. It shows how a jail rule keeps caught players in the game.

## What you need

- an open space split in two by a line
- two flags, such as scarves tied to sticks
- a spot for the jail, such as a tree or a chalk circle
- a watch

## How to play

1. **Divide.** Make two teams. Each team holds one half of the ground. Mark a boundary line between them.
2. **Place flags.** Each team has five minutes to put its flag on its own half, in view, up to 200 paces from the line. Guards may stay near, but not within 25 steps unless an enemy comes inside that circle.
3. **Cross.** At a signal, players run into the other half to take the enemy flag.
4. **Capture.** An enemy who holds you and says Caught three times has taken you prisoner. You are taken to jail.
5. **Rescue.** A teammate who touches a prisoner who is holding the jail frees both of them to go home. A rescuer can free only one prisoner at a time, and is jailed if caught first.
6. **Win.** Carry the enemy flag across the line to your own half. If the flag-carrier is tagged, the flag goes back where it was. If time runs out, the team with the most prisoners wins.

## After playing, talk about

- When did you defend, when did you raid, and when did you rescue?
- How did the jail rule change who dared to cross?
- What would you change so smaller players have a part to play?
