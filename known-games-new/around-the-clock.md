# Around the Clock — hit every number, in order

- Time: 10–15 min

Players toss coins at numbered boxes. Each player must hit box 1, then box 2, then box 3, and so on in order. Only a hit on the box you need next counts. The first to finish the whole row wins.

## What it shows a designer

A race can be made with no board path at all. It shows how a fixed order makes every player's aim the same and leaves skill the only difference between them.

## What you need

- a sheet of paper with ten boxes in a row, numbered 1 to 10
- three coins of the same size
- a line about one metre from the sheet

## How to play

1. **Draw the row.** Draw ten boxes in a row and write 1 to 10 in order, one number to a box. Put the sheet flat on a table.
2. **Pick the order.** Each player tosses one coin at the row. The coin nearest the middle of the first box goes first. Then play goes round the table.
3. **Toss.** On your turn, stand behind the line and toss three coins, one after another. Aim for the number you need next. Start with box 1.
4. **Count only the next number.** If a coin lands in the box you need, it counts, and you aim for the next number with your next coin. A coin in any other box counts for nothing.
5. **Win.** The first player to hit all ten boxes in order wins.

## After playing, talk about

- How did your aim change from box to box?
- Did you feel the order helped or held you back?
- What would change if a player could choose any box next?
