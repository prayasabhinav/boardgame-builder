# Ring Taw — knock the marbles out of the ring

- From: Ring Taw, a traditional marbles game
- Players: 2 or more players
- Time: 10–15 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Everything Games Book, p. 269
- This version: Coins or buttons flicked with a finger, in place of marbles, on a large table or on the floor, with smaller rings than the book gives

Players put counters into an inner ring and flick one more counter from a line around it. A counter knocked clear of the outer ring is kept, and the shooter shoots again. When both rings are empty, the most counters wins.

## What it shows a designer

A turn can last as long as your aim holds. It shows how the shooter's miss changes the game too, because their own counter stays in the ring for the next player to win.

## What you need

- a large sheet of paper or a clear patch of floor
- chalk or a pencil and a piece of string, to draw the rings
- about six identical counters, such as coins or buttons, for each player

## How to play

1. **Draw the rings.** Draw a small inner ring about a hand-span across. Draw an outer ring around it, about one metre across. The outer ring is the taw line.
2. **Fill the ring.** Each player puts the same number of counters, say four, into the inner ring, spaced evenly.
3. **Shoot.** On your turn, put your shooter anywhere on the taw line and flick it at the counters in the inner ring.
4. **Keep what you win.** If you knock a counter out of the outer ring, you keep it. Then you shoot again from where your shooter stopped. You may keep shooting until you fail to knock one out.
5. **Miss.** If you fail, your shooter stays where it stopped, unless it left the outer ring, in which case you pick it up. The next player takes a turn.
6. **Win.** Counters left inside either ring stay in play and are won the same way. When both rings are clear, the player with the most counters wins.

## After playing, talk about

- When did you choose to shoot from a different place on the line, and why?
- Whose turn did your missed shooter help?
- How would the game change with more counters in the ring?
