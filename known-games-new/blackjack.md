# Blackjack — get close to 21 without going over

- From: Blackjack (Twenty-one, Vingt-et-Un), a French card game
- Players: 2–10 players
- Time: 15–20 min
- Made by: Traditional; played in France for a long time
- Rules from: Hoyle's Rules of Games, under 'Blackjack (Twenty-one)'
- This version: A home version with a rotating dealer, one bet per player and no splitting, doubling or bonus pay-outs

One player is the dealer and plays against each of the others, one at a time. Each player tries to reach a total close to 21 without going over. You decide whether to take more cards or stop.

## What it shows a designer

You can stop at any time, so the risk is yours to choose. It shows how a clear rule, one number you must not pass, makes a simple pull between taking one more and staying safe.

## What you need

- a deck of 52 cards
- about 10 coins or buttons for each player

## How to play

1. **Card values.** An ace counts 1 or 11, as the holder wishes. Kings, queens, jacks and tens count 10. Other cards count their number.
2. **Bet and deal.** Each player bets chips. The dealer gives each player one card face down and one face up, and does the same for themselves.
3. **Players act.** Starting left of the dealer, each player either stands or says "Hit me" to take a card face up. You may keep taking cards. If your total goes over 21, you show your cards and the dealer takes your bet.
4. **The dealer acts.** After all have stood, the dealer may take cards too. If the dealer goes over 21, every player who stood is paid.
5. **Compare.** If the dealer stands on 21 or less, players with a higher total are paid their bet, players with the same total tie, and players with a lower total lose their bet.
6. **A natural.** An ace and a ten-count card in the first two cards is a natural and is paid two to one. That player becomes the next dealer.

## After playing, talk about

- When did you stop, and what made you sure?
- How did the dealer's shown card change your choices?
- What happened when many players had stopped before the dealer?
