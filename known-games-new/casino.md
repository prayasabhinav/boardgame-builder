# Casino — pair, add and build to capture

- From: Casino, an old card game for two
- Players: 2 players
- Time: 15–20 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Everything Games Book, pp. 60–63
- This version: Sweeps are added up at the end, and only the difference is scored

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

Two players share a row of face-up cards in the middle. On your turn you play one card from your hand. It can grab cards from the middle, or add to them, or just stay there. At the end, you count what you captured.

## What it shows a designer

The cards in the middle belong to nobody, so every move changes what the other player can take. A player can protect cards by building on them, but only if holding the card that will take them later. It shows how a shared space lets players interfere without ever attacking.

## What you need

- a deck of 52 cards
- paper and a pen for scores

## How to play

1. **Deal.** Cut for the deal; the lowest card deals (Ace is low). Give 4 cards to your opponent, then lay 4 face up in the middle, then 4 to yourself. After all hands are played, deal 4 more to each player (none to the middle). Repeat until the deck runs out.
2. **Play one card.** The player who is not the dealer goes first. Every turn you must play exactly one card from your hand. If it does not capture, build or call, it stays on the table.
3. **Pair.** Play a card that matches the number of cards on the table. You take it and every matching card there. A King takes all the Kings.
4. **Combine.** Play a card that equals the total of two or more table cards. A Two and a Six on the table can be taken by an Eight. You may take several such groups at once if each adds to the same number.
5. **Build.** Lay a card from your hand on a table card to make a new total, and say that total aloud. Put a Two on a Five and say "Seven". Next turn, take the build with a Seven, unless your opponent takes it first. You cannot raise your own build, but your opponent can raise it by adding a card from their hand.
6. **Call.** If you hold two cards of the same number and one is on the table (or in a build), play the second on it and say the number. Now only a card of that number can take the pile.
7. **Sweep.** If you take every card on the table in one move, turn the card you used face up as a sweep. If your opponent also sweeps, the two cancel. Only the difference counts.
8. **Last cards.** After the last hand is played, the player who took the last card takes whatever is left on the table.
9. **Score.** Count: Ten of Diamonds 2; Two of Spades 1; most cards 3; most Spades 1; each Ace 1; each sweep 1. The higher total wins. Equal totals are a draw.

## After playing, talk about

- When did you build instead of capturing straight away, and what were you protecting?
- How did a card you played change what your opponent could take?
- Which of the scoring cards did you chase, and which did you let go?
