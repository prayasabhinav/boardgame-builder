# Chase the Ace — pass the high card along

- From: Chase the Ace (also called Ranter-Go-Round), a card game for a crowd
- Players: 5–20 players
- Time: 10–15 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: Hoyle's Rules of Games, under 'Chase the Ace'
- This version: Played with the ace high, as in the main rules, and no second version

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

Each player gets one card, and everyone wants a low card. In turn, you may swap your card with the player on your left, who has to accept. The highest cards shown at the end pay a chip.

## What it shows a designer

A whole round fits in one card and one choice. The game gives each player a tiny decision and a forced reaction from a neighbour, so the table is busy even though nobody has a hand to manage.

## What you need

- a deck of 52 cards, with the ace as the highest card
- 3 coins or buttons for each player
- a pot space in the middle

## How to play

1. **Deal.** Give each player one card face down. Keep the rest as the stock. Everyone starts with 3 chips.
2. **Look.** Starting left of the dealer, each player looks at their card.
3. **Keep or swap.** You may keep your card or swap it with the player on your left, who has to give theirs up. Then it is that player's turn to decide.
4. **The dealer.** The dealer may keep their card or throw it away, cut the stock and take the top card of the lower pile.
5. **Show.** Everyone turns their card over. Suits do not matter. The highest card, or all tied for highest, pays one chip into the pot.
6. **Go on.** Pass the deal to the left. A player with no chips may play on once. After the next loss, that player is out. The last player left takes the pot.

## After playing, talk about

- Which card did you keep, and why?
- What did you learn about your neighbour from what they swapped?
- Did you feel any control over the result?
