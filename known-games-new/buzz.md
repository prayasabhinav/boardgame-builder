# Buzz — count, but say buzz for every five

- From: Buzz, a counting game for children
- Players: 2 or more players
- Time: 5–10 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Big Book of Rules, p. 66
- This version: Counts to 50, with Buzz Fizz added in a second game

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

Players count aloud round a circle, one number each. Instead of any number that is a multiple of five, the player says "buzz". A slip puts you out. The last player left wins.

## What it shows a designer

A single rule can turn counting into a game of attention. It shows how adding a second rule, a fizz for multiples of another number, makes the same game much harder to keep track of.

## What you need

- nothing; players sit in a circle

## How to play

1. **Count.** The first player says "one". Each player in turn says the next number.
2. **Buzz.** Instead of 5, 10, 15 and every other multiple of five, the player says "buzz".
3. **Slip.** A player who says a number when they should say "buzz", or says "buzz" when they should not, is out.
4. **Win.** The count goes up to 50, and then starts again from one if more than one player remains. The last player left wins.
5. **Buzz Fizz.** In a second game, pick two numbers such as 5 and 7. Say "buzz" for multiples of the first and "fizz" for multiples of the second. For a number that is a multiple of both, say "buzzty-fizz" or "fizzty-buzz" as you agree.

## After playing, talk about

- What helped you not slip: watching the numbers, or listening to the others?
- Where in the count did most slips happen?
- How did adding a second number change the game?
