# Table football — flick a paper ball to the far edge

- From: Table football (paper football), a desk game
- Players: 2 players
- Time: 10 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Big Book of Rules, p. 80
- This version: Played to 12 points

Two players sit at opposite ends of a table and flick a folded paper triangle towards each other's end. Getting it to hang over the edge scores most. Falling short hands the ball to the other player.

## What it shows a designer

The only thing that carries from one turn to the next is where the ball stopped. It shows how a short shot gives your opponent a good start, so the risk is part of every flick.

## What you need

- a long table
- a sheet of paper folded into a small triangle ball
- a watch or a score sheet

## How to play

1. **Make the ball.** Fold a sheet of paper in thirds the long way, then fold one corner over and over along the strip until it ends. Tuck the leftover end in.
2. **Sit.** Sit at opposite ends of the table. Choose who starts by any fair way.
3. **Flick.** Flick the ball down the table with a finger, trying to leave part of it hanging over the far edge. That is a touchdown, worth 6 points.
4. **Falls short.** If the ball stops on the table, the other player flicks it from that spot towards the opposite end.
5. **Off the side.** If the ball leaves the long edge of the table, the other player flicks it from the point where it went over.
6. **Field goal.** If the ball goes completely off the far end, the other player gets a field goal worth 3. Hold the ball upright with one finger and flick it between the first player's finger goal posts.
7. **Extra point.** After a touchdown, the scorer may try an extra point the same way, worth 1. Play to 12 points.

## After playing, talk about

- When did you flick softly to stay safe, and when did you flick hard for the edge?
- How did the place where the ball stopped change your next shot?
- Which scoring rule made you take the most risk?
