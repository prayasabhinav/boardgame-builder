# Twenty-One — get close, but not over

- From: Twenty-One, a card game
- Players: Any number of players
- Time: 10–15 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Everything Games Book, p. 166
- This version: No splitting pairs, no doubling of bets and no burnt card. Stakes are counters, never money

One player is the dealer. Everyone else plays against the dealer, trying to reach a card total as close to twenty-one as possible without going over. You decide when to ask for another card, and going over means you are out of the hand.

## What it shows a designer

Each extra card is a small bet that you will not go over. It shows how a game can be made of one repeated choice, stop or go on, and how holding your cards face down changes that choice.

## What you need

- a deck of 52 cards
- about 10 counters for each player

## How to play

1. **Card values.** Tens and court cards count ten. Other cards count their number. An Ace counts one or eleven, as its owner likes.
2. **Stakes.** Each player puts a stake of counters in front of them. The dealer also holds a bank to pay with.
3. **Deal.** The dealer gives each player two cards face down, and takes two cards too.
4. **Players draw.** Starting left of the dealer, each player looks at their cards and says "Stay" or "Hit me". On "Hit me" the dealer gives one more card face up. Keep going until you stay. If your total goes over twenty-one, you bust and your hand is out.
5. **Dealer draws.** When everyone has played, the dealer turns over their cards and may draw more. If the dealer busts, the dealer pays every player still in.
6. **Pay.** Otherwise each player still in shows their hand. A total nearer twenty-one than the dealer's wins the stake; a lower total loses it; equal totals change nothing.
7. **Natural.** An Ace and a ten-card as your first two cards is a natural twenty-one. It wins double the stake, unless the dealer also has one.

## After playing, talk about

- At what total did you stop, and what made you decide?
- How did holding your cards face down change the way you played?
- What did the dealer know that the players did not?
