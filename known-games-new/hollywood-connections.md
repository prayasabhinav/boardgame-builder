# Hollywood Connections — name the next link

- From: Hollywood Connections, a film-star word game
- Players: 2 or more players
- Time: 15 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Big Book of Rules, p. 123
- This version: Use film stars the class knows, such as Hindi film actors, and settle disputes by asking a classmate or looking at a film list on a phone

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

Players take turns naming an actor who was in a film with the actor named just before. Nobody says the film. If someone doubts a link, they can challenge it.

## What it shows a designer

The challenge turns a plain naming game into a bluffing game. It shows how a rule that lets players test each other keeps a simple call-and-response from becoming a list.

## What you need

- players who know some films and actors
- a phone or film guide to settle disputes
- paper to keep score

## How to play

1. **Agree the target.** Decide how many points end the game: 1 is very short, 3 is short, 5 is longer.
2. **Start.** The first player names an actor. The next player names another actor who has been in a film with that actor, and so on round the group. Nobody names the film.
3. **Challenge.** On your turn, you may doubt the link just made. The player who made it must name a film the two actors were both in.
4. **Score the challenge.** If the link holds, the player who made it scores a point. If the player cannot name a film, the challenger scores.
5. **Restart.** The player who scored a point starts the next round with a new actor. A name used in a link or bluff cannot be used again. The first to the agreed total wins.

## After playing, talk about

- When did you bluff, and when did you play safe?
- What did the challenge rule change about which names you chose?
- How would you settle a dispute if no book or phone were allowed?

## Its blocks

- Core mechanic: call and response
- Victory condition: most points
- Player interaction: direct confrontation
- Chance or skill: mostly skill
- Information visibility: bluffing
- Timing: take turns
