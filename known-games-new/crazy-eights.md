# Crazy Eights — match the suit or the number

- From: Crazy Eights, a card game for children and adults
- Players: 3–4 players
- Time: 10–15 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Everything Games Book, p. 69

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

Everyone is dealt cards. Players take turns putting a card on the pile. Each card must match the one before it in suit or number. Eights are wild. The first player with no cards left wins.

## What it shows a designer

A simple matching rule makes the game, and one special card breaks the rule. The wild Eight is both a rescue when you are stuck and a way to pick the suit the next player faces.

## What you need

- a deck of 52 cards (use two decks if more than four play)

## How to play

1. **Deal.** Deal one card face up to each player; the lowest card deals. Then deal the whole deck out to the players, one card at a time.
2. **Sort.** Look at your cards and group them by suit.
3. **Start.** The player left of the dealer puts one card face up in the middle.
4. **Match.** The next player must put down a card from their hand that has the same number or the same suit as the card on top.
5. **Stuck.** If you cannot match, you may skip your turn, or put down an Eight.
6. **Eights.** An Eight can be put on anything. When you play it, say which suit comes next. Pick the suit you hold most of.
7. **Win.** The first player to get rid of all their cards wins.

## After playing, talk about

- When did you save an Eight, and when did you play it as soon as you could?
- Which suit did you name, and why?
- With so many cards dealt out, how much of the game was luck?
