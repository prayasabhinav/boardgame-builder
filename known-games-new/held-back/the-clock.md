# The Clock — follow each card to its hour

- From: The Clock, a solitaire of pure chance
- Players: 1 player
- Time: 5–10 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: Hoyle's Rules of Games, under 'The Clock'
- This version: Played as in the rules, with a card taking the place of a dice roll

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

You deal thirteen face-down piles of four cards, twelve of them set in a circle like the hours on a clock. Each card you turn over sends you to the pile with its number. The game is lost when the fourth king appears.

## What it shows a designer

The card you turn tells you where to go next, like a dice roll that moves you round a board. There is nothing to decide, so it is a way to look at a game that is all chance.

## What you need

- one deck of 52 cards
- a round space on a table

## How to play

1. **Deal.** Deal the whole pack into 13 packets of four cards, face down.
2. **Set the clock.** Put twelve packets in a circle, numbered like a clock face. The thirteenth packet goes in the middle.
3. **Values.** Ace is 1, jack 11, queen 12, king 13. A king goes to the middle packet.
4. **Start.** Turn over the top card of the middle packet. Say it is a seven. Tuck it face up half under the seven packet. Then turn the top card of that packet.
5. **Carry on.** Keep tucking each card under the packet it names and turning the next. Kings go under the middle packet.
6. **End.** You win if every other card is face up before the fourth king appears. The game ends when the fourth king turns up.

## After playing, talk about

- Did any moment feel like a choice?
- How did it feel to follow the cards?
- What would you add so a player could decide something?
