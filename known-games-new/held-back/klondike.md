# Klondike — sort the pack into four suits

- From: Klondike, the best-known solitaire (also called Fascination or Demon Patience)
- Players: 1 player, or two side by side comparing results
- Time: 15–25 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: Hoyle's Rules of Games, under 'Klondike'
- This version: One player with one pack; the group can set a rule for who tries next

You deal seven piles in a staircase, with only the top card of each face up. You build the piles down in alternating colours and send each ace and its suit up to the king. Hidden cards turn over as you clear the piles above them.

## What it shows a designer

A game can be played alone against a shuffled pack. The hidden cards make each uncovered card a small surprise, and every move changes what is possible next.

## What you need

- one deck of 52 cards
- a flat table

## How to play

1. **Deal the piles.** Make seven piles left to right with 1, 2, 3 and up to 7 cards. Turn the top card of each pile face up. The rest stay face down. Keep the other 24 cards as the stock.
2. **Build down.** On a pile you may place a card one rank lower in the opposite colour, such as a red 6 on a black 7. You can move a whole run of face-up cards together.
3. **Turn cards over.** When a face-down card is left on top of a pile, turn it face up.
4. **Foundations.** Move each ace to a row above the piles. Build each suit up on its ace, in order, to the king. A card on a foundation stays there.
5. **The stock.** Turn the stock over one card at a time into a waste pile. The top waste card can be played at any time. You may go through the stock only once.
6. **Spaces.** If a pile is emptied, only a king may fill the space. You win when all four suits are built to the king.

## After playing, talk about

- Which move did you regret, and what could you not see?
- When did you wait instead of making a move you could make?
- How did the face-down cards change how you played?
