# Basic Rummy — make sets, lay them down, empty your hand

- From: Rummy, a card game that grew from the Mexican game Conquian
- Players: 2–6 players
- Time: 15–25 min
- Made by: Traditional; played across the United States after about 1850
- Rules from: Hoyle's Rules of Games, under 'Basic Rummy'
- This version: One deal decides it. The first player to use up their hand wins, and there is no payment for leftover points

You try to turn your whole hand into sets. A set is three or more cards of one rank, or three or more cards in a row in one suit. Each turn you draw one card, lay down any sets you have, and discard one card.

## What it shows a designer

Sets laid on the table can be added to by anyone. It shows how a shared pile of finished work lets one player help another without meaning to.

## What you need

- one pack of 52 playing cards
- a pen and paper if you want to keep score

## How to play

1. **Deal.** Deal 10 cards each with two players, 7 each with three or four, 6 each with five or six. Put the rest face down as the stock. Turn the top card face up beside it to start the discard pile.
2. **Draw.** The player on the dealer's left goes first. Take the top card of the stock or the top card of the discard pile.
3. **Meld.** If you can, lay sets face up on the table. A group is three or four cards of one rank. A run is three or more cards of one suit in order. In this version the ace is low only, so A-2-3 works but Q-K-A does not.
4. **Lay off.** You may add a card from your hand to any set on the table, yours or anyone else's: the fourth card of a group, or the next card in a run.
5. **Discard.** End your turn by putting one card face up on the discard pile. You do not need to discard if you laid down your last card.
6. **Win.** The first player with no cards left wins. If the stock runs out first, turn the discard pile over without shuffling to make a new stock.

## After playing, talk about

- When did you keep a card you did not need, only to stop someone else going out?
- Did laying off on another player's set help them more than it helped you?
- How did the face-up discard pile change what you could guess about others' hands?
