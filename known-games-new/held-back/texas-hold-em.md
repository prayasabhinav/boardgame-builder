# Texas Hold'em — two secret cards and five shared ones

- From: Texas Hold'em, a community card form of poker
- Players: 2–8 players
- Time: 15–25 min
- Made by: not named in the book
- Rules from: The Big Book of Rules, p. 309
- This version: Beans or coins for chips, five hands, and the player with the most chips at the end wins

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

Each player has two secret cards. Five more cards are turned up in the middle, one group at a time, and everyone may use them. Players bet after each reveal.

## What it shows a designer

The shared cards give every player part of the same information, so the game is about what you hold that the others cannot see. It shows how revealing a little at a time keeps people betting until the end.

## What you need

- a deck of 52 playing cards
- about 20 beans or coins for each player

## How to play

1. **Agree a limit.** Decide the most anyone may bet at one time.
2. **Ante and deal.** Everyone puts one chip in the pot. Deal two cards face down to each player.
3. **First bet.** Starting left of the dealer, each player passes or bets. After a bet, each in turn folds, calls or raises. If everyone passes, deal again.
4. **The flop.** Turn three cards face up in the middle. Bet again; this time a player may pass if nobody has bet.
5. **The turn.** Turn up a fourth card. Bet again.
6. **The river.** Turn up the fifth card. Bet for the last time.
7. **Show.** Players still in make the best five-card hand they can from their two cards and the five in the middle. They may use both of their own cards, one, or none. The best hand takes the pot; equal hands split it.
8. **Play on.** Play five hands. The player with the most chips wins.

## After playing, talk about

- How did the cards in the middle change what you thought of your own hand?
- When did you fold, and was it the right moment to stop?
- What would change if all five middle cards were shown at the start?
