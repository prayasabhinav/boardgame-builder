# I Spy — guess from the first letter

- From: I Spy, a guessing game from the nursery
- Players: 2 or more players
- Time: 5 to 10 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Big Book of Rules, p. 166

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

One player, the Spy, picks something everyone can see. They say its first letter. The others take turns guessing the object.

## What it shows a designer

The Spy gives only one clue and then answers yes or no. It shows how a single hint, plus a small number of guesses, can shrink a room full of objects to one.

## What you need

- a room with plenty to look at

## How to play

1. **Choose.** The Spy silently picks an object in view of everyone.
2. **Say the letter.** The Spy says, I spy with my little eye something that begins with, and the first letter.
3. **Guess.** Each player takes a turn to guess an object, such as Is it a table?
4. **Win.** The first player to guess right is the Spy next. For harder games, give a colour or a clue such as something that belongs to Bob.

## After playing, talk about

- Which guesses ruled out the most?
- How did the first letter change what you looked at?
- What would a different clue do to the game?
