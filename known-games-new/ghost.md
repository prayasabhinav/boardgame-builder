# Ghost — add a letter, but never finish the word

- From: Ghost, a spoken word game
- Players: 3–8 players
- Time: 10–15 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Big Book of Rules, p. 48
- This version: Played aloud in a circle, with a dictionary on the table to settle challenges

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

Players build a word one letter at a time, going round the circle. Nobody wants to be the one who finishes a word. When you are stuck you can bluff, challenge or give in, and each mistake earns you a letter of GHOST.

## What it shows a designer

A rule that punishes the last move changes how every earlier move is chosen. It also shows how a bluff and a challenge can sit inside a game that has no cards or pieces at all.

## What you need

- a dictionary
- paper and a pencil to keep letters

## How to play

1. **First letters.** The first player says a letter. The next player adds a letter, and the next adds another. Words of three letters or fewer do not count.
2. **Keep it alive.** From the fourth letter on, add a letter that you think does not complete a word. You must have a real word in mind that the letters could still become.
3. **Challenge.** You may challenge the player before you, asking what word they have in mind. If they have a real word, the challenger earns a letter. If they cannot name one, they earn it.
4. **Bluff or give in.** If you are stuck, you may add a letter and bluff. If you complete a word by mistake or on purpose, you earn a letter.
5. **Letters.** Letters come in the order G, H, O, S, T. A new word starts with the player to the left of the one who started the last.
6. **Out.** A player who has all five letters is out and may no longer speak in the game. The last player left wins.
7. **No proper names.** Names of people and places, and hyphenated words, are not allowed.

## After playing, talk about

- How did the fear of finishing a word change which letter you chose?
- When did you bluff, and what made you think you could get away with it?
- What could you add so a player who is out can still take part?
