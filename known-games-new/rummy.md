# Rummy — lay down sets, go out first

- From: Rummy, a family of card games
- Players: 2 or more players
- Time: 20–25 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Everything Games Book, pp. 128–129
- This version: Three hands instead of playing up to 500 points

Each player tries to make sets of three or more cards. Every turn you take a card and throw one away. When one player lays down all their cards, the others lose points for what they still hold.

## What it shows a designer

Cards on the table score, cards in your hand cost you. It shows how a rule about what counts when the game suddenly ends makes players choose between hiding what they have and putting it down.

## What you need

- a standard deck of 52 cards
- paper and a pencil to keep score

## How to play

1. **Deal.** Deal seven cards to each player. Put the rest face down as the stock and turn the top card face up beside it to start the discard pile.
2. **Know the sets.** A run is three or more cards of one suit in order. A lay is three or more cards of the same rank, such as three kings.
3. **Take a card.** On your turn, take the top card of the stock or take the whole discard pile. If you take the pile, you must use its top card in a set at once.
4. **Lay down.** Put any run or lay you can make face up in front of you. You may also add a card to a set already on the table, yours or another player's, and the points are yours.
5. **Discard.** End your turn by putting one card face up on the discard pile.
6. **Go out.** When a player has laid down every card, the hand ends.
7. **Score.** Add up the value of your cards on the table: ace 15, king, queen and jack 10, all others 5. Take away the value of the cards still in your hand.
8. **Three hands.** Play three hands. The highest total wins.

## After playing, talk about

- When did you hold a set back, and when did you lay it down at once?
- How did the pile of discards tell you what others were collecting?
- What did you do with cards you could not use?
