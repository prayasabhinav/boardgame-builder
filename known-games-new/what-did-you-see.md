# What did you see? — act out the answer together

- From: Charades for Kids, a children's party game
- Players: 6 or more players, in teams of up to 5
- Time: 10 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Everything Games Book, p. 284

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

A leader asks each team, "What did you see?" The team agrees on something moving, such as a horse trotting down the street. Then the whole team acts it out together, walks once round the room and sits. There are no points.

## What it shows a designer

A game does not need a winner to hold a group. It shows how a question and an answer, said by everyone at once, can start a game without scores or turns that eliminate anyone.

## What you need

- an open space to move in
- one leader who does not act

## How to play

1. **Make teams.** Make teams of up to five players. The number of teams does not matter.
2. **Ask.** The leader asks the first team, "What did you see?"
3. **Decide.** The team talks briefly and agrees on something they saw that has a movement, such as a bird flying or a boy running.
4. **Act.** The team says the answer aloud, for example "I saw a horse trotting down the street." Then every member acts it out and returns to their place.
5. **Next team.** The leader asks the same question of the next team. Try for different and surprising answers each time.
6. **Help.** If a team cannot think of an answer, the leader suggests one.

## After playing, talk about

- How did your team choose between two ideas?
- Which answer from another team did you enjoy watching, and why?
- What would change if the other teams had to guess the answer?
