# Go Boom — match the suit or the rank, empty your hand

- From: Go Boom, a children's card game
- Players: 2–6 players
- Time: 10–15 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: Hoyle's Rules of Games, under 'Go Boom'
- This version: Each deal is a separate game, as the book suggests for new players. For young players, take out the face cards

Everyone has seven cards. The leader plays a card, and every other player must play a card of the same suit or the same rank. The highest card of the suit led wins the trick and leads next. The first to run out of cards wins.

## What it shows a designer

Following suit or rank is the simplest form of winning tricks. It shows how a plain rule about what you may play makes you choose which cards to keep and which to throw.

## What you need

- a pack of 52 cards, or 40 cards with the face cards taken out
- a flat table

## How to play

1. **Deal.** Deal seven cards to each player, one at a time. Put the rest face down as the stock. Ace is the highest card. Without face cards, ace is the lowest.
2. **Lead.** The player to the dealer's left plays any card face up in the middle.
3. **Follow.** Each other player in turn must play a card of the same suit as the lead card, or a card of the same rank. If you cannot, draw from the stock until you can. When the stock is empty and you cannot play, you skip.
4. **Take the trick.** The highest card of the suit led wins the trick. The winner leads next. Tricks are not kept; put them in a pile to the side.
5. **Go boom.** The first player to get rid of every card wins the deal.
6. **Several deals.** Each deal is a separate game. To count points across deals, a player with cards left scores 10 for each face card, 1 for an ace and the number on other cards. The lowest total wins when someone reaches 100.

## After playing, talk about

- How did you decide which card to keep in your hand?
- When did drawing from the stock hurt you most?
- Why does winning a trick matter here when tricks score nothing?
