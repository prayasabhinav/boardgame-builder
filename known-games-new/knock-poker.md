# Knock Poker — draw, discard, and knock when you are ready

- From: Knock Poker, a Poker game that borrows from Rummy
- Players: 3–5 players
- Time: 15–25 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: Hoyle's Rules of Games, under 'Knock Poker'
- This version: Five deals, with no bonus payments, and most chips at the end wins

Each player holds five cards. On your turn you take a card from the pile or the discards, then throw one away. When you think your hand is good, you knock, and everyone gets one more turn.

## What it shows a designer

The player who knocks decides when the round ends, and the others have only one turn to answer. It shows how handing one player the power to end a round creates a gamble for everyone else.

## What you need

- one pack of 52 playing cards
- about 20 chips, coins or dried beans for each player
- a sheet that lists the hand order, highest first: straight flush, four of a kind, full house, flush, straight, three of a kind, two pairs, one pair, highest card

## How to play

1. **Ante and deal.** Everyone puts in one chip. Deal five cards face down to each player. The rest of the pack goes face down in the middle as the stock.
2. **Draw and discard.** The player on the dealer's left takes either the top card of the stock or the top discard, then puts one card from their hand face up on the discards. Turns go round to the left. Keep the discard pile squared so only the top card shows.
3. **Knock.** At any turn, after drawing and before discarding, a player may knock. They then discard as usual.
4. **One last turn.** Every other player gets one more turn to draw and discard. After it, each of them either drops out and pays the knocker one chip, or stays in.
5. **Showdown.** The knocker does not draw again. Players still in show their hands. If the knocker has the best hand, each player who stayed in pays them two chips. If someone beats the knocker, that player takes the antes and the knocker pays two chips to each player who stayed in. Tied players split the winnings.
6. **Next deal.** The deal passes left. After five deals, the player with the most chips wins.

## After playing, talk about

- When did you knock early, and when did you wait?
- How did you decide whether to stay in after someone knocked?
- What would change if the knocker also got one more draw?
