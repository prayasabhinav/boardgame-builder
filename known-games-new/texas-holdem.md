# Texas Hold'em — two cards of your own, five that everyone shares

- From: Texas Hold'em, a Poker game
- Players: 2 or more players
- Time: 20–30 min
- Made by: not named in the book
- Rules from: Hoyle's Rules of Games, under 'Texas Hold'em'
- This version: Five deals, a fixed bet size of 2 chips, and most chips at the end wins

Each player holds two cards that no one else sees. Five more cards go face up in the middle, and everyone may use them. You make the best five-card hand from your two cards and the five shared ones.

## What it shows a designer

Shared cards give every player the same information, and private cards give each a secret. It shows how the mix of the two lets people guess what others hold from what is on the table.

## What you need

- one pack of 52 playing cards
- about 30 chips, coins or dried beans for each player
- a sheet that lists the hand order, highest first: straight flush, four of a kind, full house, flush, straight, three of a kind, two pairs, one pair, highest card

## How to play

1. **Blinds.** The player left of the dealer puts in 1 chip, and the next player puts in 2. These are forced bets that make a pot to fight over.
2. **Deal.** Give each player two cards face down. Starting left of the second blind, each player may fold (drop out), call (match the 2 chips) or raise (add 2 more).
3. **The flop.** Put three cards face up in the middle. Starting on the dealer's left, players still in bet again, until everyone has put in the same amount.
4. **The turn.** Put a fourth card face up. Bet again.
5. **The river.** Put a fifth card face up. Bet one last time.
6. **Showdown.** If only one player is left, they take the pot. Otherwise players still in show their cards, and the best five-card hand from their two cards and the five shared ones takes the pot. Ties share it.
7. **Next deal.** The dealer's seat moves left. After five deals, the player with the most chips wins.

## After playing, talk about

- How did each new shared card change what you thought the others held?
- When was a bet about your own cards, and when was it about the story you were telling?
- What would change if there were only four shared cards?
