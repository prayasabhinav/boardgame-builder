# Spoil Five — win three tricks first, or spoil it for everyone

- From: Spoil Five, an old card game
- Players: 3 to 10 players, best with five
- Time: 15–20 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Everything Games Book, p. 162
- This version: No robbing, no reneging and no jinking. Below the top four trumps, cards rank Ace high to Two low

Each player holds five cards. One suit is trumps. Players play one card each in turn, and the highest card takes the trick. The first to win three tricks takes the pool. If nobody does, the hand is spoiled, everyone adds to the pool and the cards are dealt again.

## What it shows a designer

A race to a small number can be blocked. It shows how a growing prize turns a failed round into a reason to try again, and how winning a trick matters less than who wins the third.

## What you need

- a deck of 52 cards
- about 10 counters for each player

## How to play

1. **Pool.** Every player puts one counter in the pool.
2. **Deal.** Give each player five cards, two at a time and then three. Turn the next card face up: its suit is trumps. The player to the dealer's left leads.
3. **Top trumps.** In the trump suit these cards rank highest, in this order: the Five, the Jack, the Ace of Hearts, then the Ace of the trump suit. The Ace of Hearts counts as a trump even when Hearts are not trumps. Other cards rank Ace high to Two low.
4. **Play a trick.** The leader plays any card. Each other player plays one card. You follow the suit led if you can, or play a trump. Trumps beat every other suit. The highest card of the winning kind takes the trick, and its winner leads next.
5. **Win.** The first player to win three tricks takes the whole pool and the hand ends.
6. **Spoil.** If all five tricks are played and nobody has three, the hand is spoiled. Every player adds one more counter to the pool and the next dealer deals again.

## After playing, talk about

- When did you stop trying to win a trick and play to stop someone else?
- What did the growing pool do to how hard players fought?
- Which cards did you count on, and how did you know what was still out?
