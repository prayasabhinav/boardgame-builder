# Speculation — buy and sell the winning card

- From: Speculation, a card game of trading
- Players: 5–9 players
- Time: 15–20 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: Hoyle's Rules of Games, under 'Speculation'
- This version: Played for one pool, with no buying of unturned cards unless the players agree

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

Each player has three face-down cards in a pile. A trump suit is set by a turned-up card. Players turn their cards one by one, and whoever holds the highest trump card when all are turned wins the pool. Any new high trump can be sold to another player.

## What it shows a designer

Nothing in your pile can be changed, but its worth can be traded. The game turns a card into a price. It shows how a chance game becomes a talking game when players are allowed to buy and sell.

## What you need

- a deck of 52 cards
- about 8 coins or buttons for each player
- a pot space in the middle

## How to play

1. **Ante.** Each player puts one chip into the pot. The dealer puts in two.
2. **Deal.** Deal 3 cards to each player, face down, in one pile, without looking. Turn up one more card. Its suit is trumps, and it belongs to the dealer.
3. **An ace.** If the turned-up card is an ace, the dealer takes the pool at once and the deal passes left.
4. **Sell it.** Otherwise the dealer may keep the turned-up card or sell it to the highest bidder. The money goes to the dealer. The buyer puts it face up in front of them.
5. **Turn cards.** Starting left of the dealer, each player in turn turns the top card of their own pile. When a trump is turned that is higher than the highest one showing, its owner may keep it or sell it.
6. **Win.** Carry on until every card is turned. The owner of the highest trump takes the pool.

## After playing, talk about

- What made you pay more for a card than it was worth?
- When did you sell, and what did you know that the buyer did not?
- Who had control over the result?
