# Hearts — take as few hearts as you can

- From: Hearts, a trick-taking card game
- Players: 4 players
- Time: 20 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Everything Games Book, pp. 110–111
- This version: Four deals with chips, and the pool goes to the player with the fewest hearts (the book's rules for taking all thirteen are left out)

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

Four players each play a card in turn. The highest card of the suit led wins the trick. The trouble is that tricks holding hearts cost chips, so you want to win as few of them as you can.

## What it shows a designer

In most trick games you want tricks. Here you want to avoid them, so a high card is a danger and a low card is safe. It shows how turning one goal upside down changes every choice.

## What you need

- a standard deck of 52 cards
- about 15 chips or coins for each player
- a dish in the middle for the pool

## How to play

1. **Deal.** Deal all 52 cards, one at a time, so each player has 13. Aces are highest and twos lowest. There is no trump suit.
2. **Lead.** The player to the dealer's left leads any card. Everyone else plays one card in turn.
3. **Follow suit.** If you have a card of the suit led, you play one. If you have none, you may play any card you like. This is how you get rid of hearts.
4. **Win the trick.** The highest card of the suit led wins the trick. That player collects the cards and leads the next trick. Play all 13 tricks.
5. **Count hearts.** Each player counts the hearts in the tricks they took. For each heart, put one chip in the pool.
6. **Share out.** The player who took the fewest hearts takes the pool. If two or more tie, they share it. Pass the deal to the left.
7. **Four deals.** Play four deals. The player with the most chips at the end wins.

## After playing, talk about

- When did you want to lose a trick, and how did you make sure you did?
- How did you work out who was short of a suit?
- What changed once the hearts were nearly all played?
