# Simon Says — obey only when Simon says so

- From: Simon Says, a children's party game
- Players: 3–30 players
- Time: 5–10 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: The Everything Games Book, p. 280
- This version: Played standing or sitting at desks, with small movements such as touching your head or raising an arm

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

One player is Simon and gives orders for movements. Everyone else copies a movement only when the order begins with the words 'Simon says'. Anyone who moves on an order without those words, or who is slow to move on one with them, is out.

## What it shows a designer

A rule can turn a simple call into a trap. It shows how one small condition on a command makes every player watch the words as well as the movement.

## What you need

- a clear space, or a classroom where everyone can stand beside a desk
- one player to be Simon

## How to play

1. **Choose Simon.** One player is Simon and stands facing the others. Everyone else stands in rows where Simon can see them.
2. **Give an order.** Simon calls a movement and does it, for example 'Simon says touch your nose'. Everyone else copies it at once.
3. **Leave out the words.** Sometimes Simon gives an order without 'Simon says' and still does the movement. Nobody may copy it.
4. **Go out.** A player is out if they copy an order that did not start with 'Simon says', or if they are slow to obey an order that did. Out players sit down.
5. **Win.** Keep going until one player is left standing. That player wins. In the variation Ducks Fly, Simon names animals and arms are raised only for animals that can fly.

## After playing, talk about

- Which orders caught the most players out, and why?
- Did watching Simon's body help you or fool you?
- What would change if out players were not sent to sit down?
