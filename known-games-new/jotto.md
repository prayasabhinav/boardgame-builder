# Jotto — guess the secret word

- From: Jotto, a pencil-and-paper word game sold as a boxed game in the 1950s
- Players: 2 players
- Time: 10–20 min
- Made by: Traditional; first sold by Selchow & Righter in the 1950s
- Rules from: New Rules for Classic Games, p. 10
- This version: Each player may tell one lie during the game (the book's One-Lie Jotto), which is optional

Each player secretly writes a five-letter word. Players take turns guessing each other's word. After each guess, the owner of the word says only how many letters of the guess are also in the secret word. First to name the other's word wins.

## What it shows a designer

A reply that only gives a number leaves the guesser to work out the rest. It shows how a small amount of information, given every turn, builds into a clear picture, and how allowing one lie turns every answer into something to check.

## What you need

- a sheet of paper and a pencil for each player, to write the secret word and keep notes
- an agreement between players on what counts as a word, such as a dictionary one of you can check

## How to play

1. **Choose a word.** In secret, each player writes a five-letter word on their own paper. No letter may appear twice in it.
2. **Guess.** On your turn, say a five-letter word out loud. It also may not repeat a letter. Write it down with the answer.
3. **Answer.** The other player counts how many letters of your guess also appear in their secret word, in any position, and says the number.
4. **Watch for anagrams.** An answer of five does not mean you found the word. LARGE and GLARE use the same letters. Keep guessing until you name the exact word.
5. **One lie each.** Each player may give one wrong number at any time in the game, early or late. Say before you start that this rule is on. You never have to use it.
6. **Win.** The first player to say the other's exact secret word wins.

## After playing, talk about

- Which guess told you the most, and which told you almost nothing?
- Did you pick a secret word with anagrams on purpose? What did that give away?
- When did you suspect a lie, and what made you think so?
