# Martinetti — climb to twelve and back with three dice

- From: Martinetti, an old dice game
- Players: 2 or more players
- Time: 10–15 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: Board and Table Games from Many Civilizations, p. 134
- This version: On the way back, a player needs 11, then 10, and so on down to 1, since the book only says the marker goes from 1 to 12 and back to 1

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

Each player has a marker on a strip numbered 1 to 12. You throw three dice and move your marker up one space each time the dice give you the next number you need. The first player to climb to 12 and come back to 1 wins.

## What it shows a designer

A throw of dice can be read more than one way: as single numbers or as sums. It shows how letting a player choose how to use a roll gives a little control, and how letting others grab a missed number keeps everyone watching.

## What you need

- a strip of paper with 12 boxes numbered 1 to 12
- one counter for each player
- 3 dice

## How to play

1. **Set up.** Lay out the strip. Each player has a counter, which starts off the strip.
2. **Order.** Each player throws the three dice. The highest score goes first and the lowest goes last. Ties throw again.
3. **Throw.** On your turn, throw all three dice. Your counter needs a 1 to start. If a die shows the number you need, move up one space. You can also add two or three dice together to make the number you need.
4. **Use more of the throw.** One throw can move you more than once. A throw of 1, 2 and 3 takes you from the start to space 3, because the dice give 1, then 2, then 3. A throw of 1, 4 and 6 can make 5, 7, 10 or 11.
5. **Keep going or pass.** While a throw moves you, throw again. When a throw gives you nothing, pass the dice to the player on your left.
6. **Call it.** If you miss a number you could have used, any other player who needs it can call it out before the dice are thrown again, and move up. If two players call together, the one nearest your left goes first.
7. **Win.** The first player to reach 12 and come back to 1 wins.

## After playing, talk about

- When did you choose to add dice together, and when did you use them one by one?
- What changed when other players could call out a number you missed?
- How much of your result came from the dice, and how much from reading them?
