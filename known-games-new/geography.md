# Geography — name a place that starts with the last letter

- From: Geography, a spoken word game
- Players: Any number of players
- Time: 10–15 min
- Made by: Traditional; played on long drives and quiet evenings
- Rules from: The Everything Games Book, p. 285
- This version: Played aloud in a circle, with a map or atlas on the table to settle doubts

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

Players take turns naming a place: a town, city, state, country, river or mountain range. Each new place must start with the last letter of the one before. A place can be named only once. Anyone who is stuck or repeats a place is out.

## What it shows a designer

A list that grows on its own, with no board, can still push players out one by one. It shows how a short rule on letters, plus a memory task, makes a game that anyone can start in a car.

## What you need

- a map or atlas, to check places
- nothing else

## How to play

1. **First place.** One player names any place on the map, such as a town, state or river.
2. **Next place.** The player on their left names a different place that begins with the last letter of the one just said.
3. **No repeats.** A place that has been named cannot be named again. Every player keeps track in their head.
4. **Stuck or repeating.** A player who cannot think of a place, or who repeats one, is out of the game.
5. **Winner.** The last player left in the game wins.
6. **Add limits.** To make it harder, allow only one kind of place, such as only countries, or give each turn a time limit.

## After playing, talk about

- Which letters made the game harder, and what did you do about them?
- How did remembering the places already named change what you said?
- What could you change so a player who is out can still take part?
