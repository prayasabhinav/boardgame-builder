# Suit of Armor — collect seven cards of one suit

- From: Suit of Armor, a card passing game
- Players: 3–7 players
- Time: 5–10 min
- Made by: Traditional; also called Hay Ship Sails
- Rules from: The Big Book of Rules, p. 340
- This version: One round with a count of seven cards in a suit

Made with boardgameBuilder 1.x. To design from it, open boardgameBuilder and choose Open game: its blocks go on the canvas, and the feelings are left for you to pick.

Each player holds seven cards. Everyone passes one unwanted card to the left and picks up the one passed to them. The first to hold seven of one suit wins.

## What it shows a designer

Every player is giving away what they do not want, which is what the next player may need. It shows how a game made only of passing can still decide who wins by what you choose to keep.

## What you need

- a deck of 52 playing cards

## How to play

1. **Deal.** Deal seven cards to each player. The rest of the deck is not used.
2. **Choose a suit.** Look at your cards and choose a suit you hold most of.
3. **Pass.** The dealer puts one unwanted card face down on their left. The player on their left puts down one of their own and picks up the dealer's card. This goes round the table.
4. **Keep passing.** Keep going round. You may change your chosen suit if your cards change.
5. **Win.** The first player with seven cards of one suit calls 'Suit of Armor!' and wins.

## After playing, talk about

- When did you change your suit, and why?
- What did you pass that someone else needed?
- How could you tell what another player was collecting?
