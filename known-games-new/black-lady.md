# Black Lady — pass your danger cards and dodge the Queen of Spades

- From: Hearts, a card game; Black Lady is its most played form
- Players: 4 players
- Time: 15–25 min
- Made by: Traditional
- Rules from: Hoyle's Rules of Games, under 'Black Lady'
- This version: Four deals, and the player with the lowest total of penalty points after the fourth deal wins

This is Hearts with one big danger card, the Queen of Spades, called the Black Lady, which is worth 13 penalty points. Before play, every player passes three cards to their left. You try to take as few penalty points as you can.

## What it shows a designer

The three-card pass gives every player a decision before the first trick: which of my cards are a danger to me, and is it safe to hand them on. It shows how a choice made before play begins can matter as much as the play itself, and how one card can change the whole table.

## What you need

- one pack of 52 playing cards
- paper and a pen for the score

## How to play

1. **Deal.** The dealer gives out the whole pack, one card at a time, so each player has thirteen cards.
2. **Pass three.** Each player picks three cards and puts them face down in front of the player on their left. Do this before you pick up the cards you are given.
3. **Lead and follow.** The player to the dealer's left leads any card. Everyone must follow suit if they can, and otherwise may play any card. The highest card of the suit led wins the trick, and there are no trumps. The winner leads next.
4. **Count penalties.** Each heart you take in a trick counts 1 point against you. The Queen of Spades counts 13 against you.
5. **Take all.** A player who wins all thirteen hearts and the Queen of Spades scores nothing for the deal, and everyone else also scores nothing.
6. **Play four deals.** Pass the deal to the left and add up penalty points. The lowest total wins.

## After playing, talk about

- Which cards did you pass, and what did you choose to keep?
- Did you try to take everything, and why or why not?
- How did one card, the Queen of Spades, change how everyone played?
