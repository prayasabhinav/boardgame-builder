idea: Let the other player pick which move you make | In Refusal Chess a player may reject the opponent's move once, so the opponent has to play a different one. idea: A piece with unlimited power is limited by its twin | In Emperor King Chess each king may jump to any square, but not onto a defended enemy emperor. They cancel each other, so a piece cannot simply be picked off, and the end game turns on attacking the emperor's last defender. | p. 195
