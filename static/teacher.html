Your class

Start a class and give your students the code or the link. Whenever a student's canvas holds a marked pair, they appear here with the pair, why it is marked and the rest of their blocks, so you know whom to look in on. Placing a marked pair stays open to them.

  1. Press Start a class.
  2. Students open the link, or open boardgameBuilder, press Join a class in the header and type the code.
  3. They type their name and design as usual.
