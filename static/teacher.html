Start a class

You get a five-letter code. Give it to your students, or the link.

  1. Press Start a class.
  2. Students open the link, or open boardgameBuilder and press Join a class in the header.
  3. They type their name and design as usual.

Teacher's page

Your class

Sometimes a student puts two blocks on their canvas that contradict each other: chase and tag, where everyone runs at once, with take turns, where players go one at a time. The game cannot do both. The tool still lets them, marks both blocks unusual on their screen, and shows them here with the two blocks, why they clash and the rest of their design, so you know whom to talk to. It may be a mistake, or the new idea in their game.
