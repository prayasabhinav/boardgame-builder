How it works

Design a board game, one turn at a time

  1. Say how the game should feel. Drag a feeling, such as “tense” or “together”, from the left onto the shelf at the top.
  2. Build the game. Drag blocks into the slots. Each block fits only its own slot. Core mechanic and Simulation take as many blocks as you like; every other slot takes one, and a new block replaces the old one.
  3. Press Iterate. The tool looks up what your blocks tend to do together in a list prepared in advance, and says what is taking shape. Nothing is generated while you play.
  4. Change something and press Iterate again. Each press is one turn. When you are done, press Mark complete for a closing summary.

Press ? on any block to see what it means. Your design stays in this browser; Start fresh clears it.
