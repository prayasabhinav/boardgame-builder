Propose a game

A known game is a short game people can play with things at hand: paper, dice, coins, a deck of cards, or nothing. Each one is a Markdown file in one shape, the same file the tool and this wiki hand out. To propose a new game, start from the blank file or from any known game's file, write your game into it, and send it here. To propose a change to a known game, download its file, change it, and send it back with the same name.

Write enough for someone who has never seen the game to play it: what to lay out, who starts, what a turn is, how it ends, who wins, and what happens on a tie. Say too what it shows a designer: the known games are here to teach, so a game earns its place by making one design idea felt.

An editor reads each proposal. Until then only you and the editors can see it. If it is approved, it appears on the wiki under your name, marked as proposed, and it reaches the tool the next time approved proposals are carried in, without an animation at first.
